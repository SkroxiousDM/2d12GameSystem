### Changed — Frontier sunlight and restricted staking (2026-11-08)

- Sun-Sick now caps ordinary gains/stacking at 5, where collapse causes Taken Out without a Scar or Dying. Full-day exposure at 5 advances to 6: dhampirs use core Sōma Scar limits and Dying only with lethal stakes; removal from harmful sunlight stabilises, and recovery below 6 after exposure ends clears the sunlight-caused clock, not the Scar. Separate injuries retain their consequences. Vampires instead suffer automatic sunlight destruction without a roll or Dying clock (`e6e38d14b8d040ffc2cceb71aacd662018dc818e`).
- Awaken the Blood's physical-status ceiling expressly permits that full-day Sun-Sick progression, without changing its other benefits or costs (`03f7400a59cc07edc7b6232325b324271b30d2df`).
- Staking remains an untiered condition, not a killing method. Its restricted equivalence meets sustained fire's broken/tier-5 prerequisite and supplies −15 to subsequent killing-strike Difficulty, or the reduction from a higher actual negative status instead, never both; eligible ability reductions remain additional. No other status-tier, Blood Debt, recovery or ability-trigger equivalence, automatic strike or destruction is granted. Extra stakes add no benefit; removal ends the condition and equivalences, not actual statuses or settled outcomes (`45485c2fab28400458cb97de86ec55fc3b272c67`).
- The sunlight killing-method table and killing-strike staking cross-reference were reconciled (`20d4880ad9c0115ecde2c38e54dc67e57dd95491`, `7b1734d6ebfef22d6c770739caf484af667c8ca9`). Frontier sheet summaries (`d43b314e636ecdce804e90524a7132eeec755d77`), Vampire Kind Hard to Kill/Blood Debt summaries (`36d736f093eeaf6fa47fd7fc98a48d3b48e41eb8`) and the setting Quick Reference (`70caa3b80fc8e43824d213e143eef60f0c23f516`) now record the scoped rules.
- Phase 1 decisions and the implementation tracker now distinguish these implemented selections from broader pending compatibility work. Earlier dated entries and linked changelog history are preserved. This is unreleased source/documentation work present on main, not Phase 1 completion or a published release. Automated PDF-update commits exist, but workflow/build diagnostics, PDF contents, rendered sheet/Quick Reference fit, balance and playtesting have not been verified by this reconciliation.

### Changed — Adversaries procedures and Frontier setting neutrality (2026-11-07)

- Adversaries Boss Phases now use explicit fixed designation, Rank-based Special allocation, fixed shared-Disposition thresholds, final eligible end-of-volley checks and next-volley benefits. Desperate rating precedence, participation, suspension and lawful-return boundaries are explicit. Related summaries now follow settled core versus doubles/Woe, Power-spending, simultaneous setup-defeat and reinforcement recovery/compromise rules (`fef2b6f9f2d89ebe6dc5f38c8f5746b05ff484de`).
- The core Adversaries chapter was split first into two source files (`e5700156c7bffa1c4285392c38b673f8a50f67cc`), then into three (`b833e4097d81ec9b45a1a15e2cfa288478965816`). The original filename remains the entry point; it passes the statblock helper explicitly to part two, which loads part three. Existing section numbers and reading order are retained. Split-source preservation and committed file hashes were checked; this is not rendered-layout validation.
- Last Stand (§6.10) costs 2 Woe and postpones eligible defeat for exactly one final volley for both sides under normal scripting and rotation. Pending defeat cannot be rescued through Disposition recovery or reinforcement Grit. Extensions cannot chain, refresh exchange-limited abilities or activate a new phase.
- Fail-safe (§6.11) remains a separate 3-Woe final-defeat compromise effect. An eligible active Guardian detonates after any Last Stand extension and before finishing procedures, assigning burned-3 to PCs within established reach and destroying the Guardian. The compromise injuries are settled afterward, without early application to lower finishing Difficulty, an automatic compromise-grade increase or duplicate injury credit.
- Deferred Special injuries (§6.12) resolve separately paid applications in trigger order after the final outcome and permitted finishing procedures, regardless of victory, defeat, mutual defeat or concession. Pending records are not active statuses. Eligible Resistance/reduction precedes stacking; remaining injuries may subsequently receive eligible one-time compromise credit. Feed and Shock Field were integrated in `b833e4097d81ec9b45a1a15e2cfa288478965816`; Venom / Infection was reconciled in `6f9a68307a1f2d98fc950cb9b2077a64e15a8467`, with no second application from compromise and poison progression only once inflicted.
- Vampire construction exceptions, the Vampire Kind package and its Specials, and retainer-clan Shadow-meld/Beast Form were relocated from core Adversaries to `docs/TestamentOfDust/tod-adversaries.typ`, Frontier Rules §16 (`ad6680cdadde890d9e73c48b9fd9dca53076976f`). The new fragment is included once through the existing Frontier Rules assembly. Core retains the generic procedures and setting-supplied Kind boundaries. Feed retains its deferred Bled-2, outside-conflict effect and recovery when used; listed costs, tiers and Grit accounting are preserved.
- Frontier Playbooks now explicitly replace the corresponding six generic playbooks rather than adding parallel choices. Mutant, Cyborg Hunter and Warden remain additional Frontier options. Generic references do not grant access to the replaced ability lists (`ba1e55f54e71bccfed1162e0702e539cd7565986`).
- Phase 1 decision 9 and its implementation tracker now allow Walks Between Worlds in Testament of Dust under its technological interpretation, superseding the earlier exclusion. The Focus's mechanics and active setting-source eligibility were not changed (`a3e61c5c397ba226b398c29bdc704e3c806dfbcb`).
- Implementation records were reconciled for the Adversaries procedures in `d785d18402396c4702e739df3a18b8d5fd1b2eb6`. This entry and the accompanying tracker follow-up record the later setting-neutrality work without marking Phase 1 complete.
- These are scoped changes on `draft/conflict-system-rewrite`, not a merged or built release. Descriptor-reference and broader active-source/setting sweeps, remaining summary reconciliation, encounter calibration, compilation, PDF rebuilding, layout verification and playtesting remain pending.

### Changed — Scripted conflict implementation and compatibility reconciliation (2026-11-07)
- **Core Rules §10:** integrated the scripted-conflict rewrite and shared procedures in active Typst source (`ff43f679ca56a3ff53af183b8f0190529671af4c`). Current setup is floor((2d12 + Disposition skill + highest Edge in its pool) / 3), then +1 per eligible helper and flat modifiers at full value, minimum 1 before opposing Superiority. Superiority alone may reduce the result to 0. This supersedes the 2026-10-18 second-halving formula and minimum-1 Superiority wording below; those entries remain historical records. Shared Disposition is not health and has no individual shares or spill-over.
- **Batch 51, Core Rules §§2, 5 and 10:** setup Help is free and committed before the leader rolls, without an ordinary Help die or duplicate free-Help benefit. Versus tests compare totals even for exceptional doubles; winning double 12s add +2 Power instead of the ordinary +1 critical, while successful double 1s retain their complication. Versus losses, ties and trumped actions generate no ordinary-failure Woe; independent failure and separately triggered complications/costs retain their treatment (`b5b1827d42f6fad7b008d2ef7c222b0c46edf8f1`, `f9c2ca265b6231843854ca1ef6c95d46c6bb6c4f`).
- **Effects and conflict spending:** action-specific supplementary permissions and purchase limits now govern mixed spending. No primary purchase is mandatory; ordinary Attack/Feint loss requires at least 1 committed Power, with the +1 base loss once per action. Winning versus prevents the opposed normal effect even when all Power funds permitted supplementary Effects. Disrupt, Advance, Weaken and Extra Feat cannot bypass committed scripts, disputed objectives or protected payments. Status permission, Power and Disposition loss remain distinct.
- **Source assembly:** `chapter-2.10-conflict-system.typ` contains the introduction and §§10.1–10.7 and includes `chapter-2.10-conflict-system-aftermath.typ` for §§10.8–10.16. Area Effects and Interpose remain in their separate source at §§10.17–10.18. The split does not renumber sections or require a duplicate parent include (`b5b1827d42f6fad7b008d2ef7c222b0c46edf8f1`).
- **Batch 52, Generic Playbooks §4.1 Furcifer:** Opportunist and Deadly Precision share a participant-specific opening from relevant unawareness or pending Impede, checked before resolution. Opportunist grants +1 permitted Power once on success. Deadly Precision chooses +1 ordinary Attack Disposition loss with committed Power, or one qualifying single-target status purchase at half purchased tier rounded up; no Feint benefit, new status permission or discount to a shared area-status purchase. Stacking, automatic-success timing and finishing exclusions are explicit; the combined offensive increase remains unplaytested (`ca671c6a1e27c4a433cd9209e13a69658ec527c8`).
- **Named ability replacements:** Second Wind/Command (`50b06b39b200c16856299d659c0d4baeb8a6a770`), Smooth Recovery (`8583a3131ea8aaac96a3421c1631cc406ddff5d0`), withdrawal/evasion/escape (`b0c2c252c5770e1deff6f47f44ee865001f9ff77`), entry/identity/Taken Out interventions (`91464fd6d937fb59bf0b69a789bf3556f3807bc7`), and Borrowed Might/Ward the Threshold/The Reveal/Immunity (`76e59ce5762752b515640d2aabbbbbed7dc3cd26`) have scoped source replacements. These named exceptions are not blanket overrides for other abilities or creature finishing procedures.
- **Summaries and records:** core Quick Reference reconciled with Batches 51–52 (`ec778fc22c48789df1245cf828352835c2ec4a37`); compatibility ledger extended (`ceea15187019ba2d9ed34f9d5effd49e27945b0f`); dated ability audit and drafts README reconciled while preserving historical findings (`630f5367ab9eb56fa9fe08663a9a25c451299137`); Batch 51 decision status corrected (`41b8a5dfafc36b84c17d95a21ff313813b4f2ed2`). The ledger retains the detailed batches 13–49 compatibility record; this entry does not certify every affected source or repeat every historical clarification.
- These are integrations on `draft/conflict-system-rewrite`, not a merged or built release. Remaining reference/shared-guidance and setting checks, active-source sweeps, numerical recalibration, Typst compilation, PDF rebuilds, layout inspection and playtesting remain pending. Existing Legacy retirement, Bled and killing-strike records below remain in force; unrelated deferred rulings are not resolved by this reconciliation.
