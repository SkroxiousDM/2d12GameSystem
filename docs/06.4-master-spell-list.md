# Master Spell List

This chapter collects every battlefield spell in the game into a single list. Each spell is a working that more than one magical tradition has discovered, inherited, stolen, or been taught — but no spell is known to all of them. A tradition's spellbook is simply the set of spells on this list that carry its name.

## How to Read This List

Every spell profile follows the format described in the Magic chapter, with one addition: a **Traditions** line naming each tradition that can teach the spell. Where a tradition knows the working under its own name — the Hammer of Thunraz, the Bounties of the Dagda, the Curse of Loki — that name is given in parentheses. The mechanics are identical regardless of the name; a Christian holy woman calling on a saint and a runeweaver cutting Laguz into ash-wood are performing the same working through different doors.

**Traditional spellcasters** may learn only spells whose Traditions line includes their own tradition. **Research spellcasters** may learn any spell on this list, subject to the GM's judgment about access to sources — though a working known to many traditions is far easier to find than one guarded by a single forbidden cult. **Dabblers** may attempt any spell, learning it flawed as usual.

## The Thirteen Traditions

The nine historical traditions of the year 500 are described in the Magic chapter and the setting documents; the four cult traditions guard the forbidden magic of the Hidden Wars.

- **Greco-Roman (GRE):** augurs, oracles, hierophants, and goētes — auspices, curse tablets, fate-magic, and the surviving mystery rites.
- **Christian (CHR):** miracle-working through faith, the names of God, and the intercession of the saints. Healing, protection, and the casting-out of unclean spirits; the Church frowns on divination and cursing.
- **Coptic (COP):** the syncretic scribe-magic of Egypt — curse texts, amulets, and old gods invoked under new names alongside the angels.
- **Germanic (GER):** runeweavers and seiðkonur — runes as sacred powers, battle-fury, and the weaving of fate.
- **Celtic (CEL):** druids and vates — the magic of land, season, healing, and the hollow hills, marked in Ogham.
- **Hermetic (HER):** philosopher-magicians and alchemists — theurgy, the astral journey, transmutation, and the summoning of servitor spirits.
- **Zoroastrian (ZOR):** the Mobedān — sacred fire, purity, the stars, and open war against the druj.
- **Jewish Babylonian (JUD):** bowl-writers and scribes — incantation bowls, seals, and the binding of demons by their hidden names.
- **Mithraic (MIT):** the soldier's mysteries of the Unconquered Sun — oaths, solar light, and sentinel-work against the Outer Dark, now passing into the Brotherhood of St. George.
- **Cult of Yog-Sothoth (YOG):** gates, thresholds, and forbidden knowledge.
- **Cult of Nyarlathotep (NYA):** illusion, delusion, dream, and the Black Sun.
- **Cult of Mormo (MOR):** corrupted fertility — rot, plague, and monstrous birth.
- **Cult of Cthulhu (CTH):** the dreaming water-magic of the Deep Ones.

## **Aegis of the Faithful**

Whether raised as a shimmering rune-ward, a shield of light called down by prayer, or a soldier's oath sworn under the sun, this working wraps its targets in an unseen defence. Blows that should land go astray, malice slides from its mark, and the faithful stand fast where others would fall.

**Traditions:** Germanic (the Bounty of Baldr), Christian, Mithraic, Jewish Babylonian

* **Skill:** Resilience
* **Difficulty:** Difficult
* **Cost:** 3 (causes Drain on Complication)
* **Duration:** A number of rounds equal to the spellcaster's Pneuma rating
* **Category:** Ward spell

**Spell Effect:** While this spell remains in effect, any attacks made against the spellcaster or their allies within Close range increase in difficulty by +2.

**Success Levels:** All affected characters gain +X Cover resistance, where X equals the number of Success Levels spent.

## **The Alchemist's Touch**

Mercury weeps, base metal pales, water clouds and sweetens: the alchemist reads the hidden sympathies between substances and briefly persuades one thing to remember it might have been another. Iron recalls a former life as lead; well-water briefly recalls the taste of wine.

**Traditions:** Hermetic (signature), Coptic, Cult of Yog-Sothoth

* **Skill:** Academia
* **Difficulty:** Difficult
* **Cost:** 3 (causes Drain on Complication)
* **Duration:** The scene
* **Category:** Transmutation spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster touches one non-living material object or quantity of substance within Reach, up to roughly a person's own mass, and transmutes it into another substance of similar mass and rough form (iron to lead, water to wine, wood to glass, and similar). The transmutation ends automatically when the scene ends, and the object reverts to its original substance.

**Success Levels:**

* For 3 Success Levels, the transmutation becomes permanent rather than ending with the scene.
* For 3 Success Levels, the target substance may instead be a precious metal or similarly valuable material; treat any Complication generated by this casting as a ruinous one at the GM's discretion — the transmutation may unravel violently, draw unwanted attention, or curdle into something worthless.

## **Augury**

Every tradition reads the future in its own way — the flight and cry of birds, the casting of rune-marked staves, the shapes in burning coals, the entrails of a sacrificial beast. Whatever the method, the diviner interprets the signs to learn whether the omens favour the course ahead or warn against it.

**Traditions:** Greco-Roman, Germanic, Celtic, Zoroastrian, Jewish Babylonian, Hermetic

* **Skill:** Observation
* **Difficulty:** Difficult
* **Cost:** 3 (causes Drain on Complication)
* **Duration:** Up to twenty-four hours
* **Category:** Manifestation spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster spends ten minutes consulting their chosen method of divination to determine whether the omens are favourable or unfavourable. The GM determines which of the following applies:

* **Auspicious:** Fortune favours the current course of action. For the duration of the spell, the spellcaster may re-roll any number of dice from a single skill test, after which the spell ends.
* **Inauspicious:** The current course of action runs against the fates — but the diviner is forewarned. For the duration of the spell, when the GM spends Doom to Create a Complication or via the Change of Circumstance option, the spellcaster may ignore the effects of that spend, after which the spell ends.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, the duration increases by twenty-four hours.
* For 2 Success Levels, the spellcaster — rather than the GM — determines the choice of omens.

## **The Binding Tablet**

The curse-writer scratches a victim's name into a strip of lead by lamplight, calls the infernal powers below to witness, and nails the tablet face-down into a grave, a well, or the roots of a dead tree. So bound, the named one finds their own hands and tongue turning traitor at every crucial moment.

**Traditions:** Greco-Roman (the Defixio), Celtic, Coptic

* **Skill:** Manipulation
* **Difficulty:** Hard, opposed by the target's Willpower + Resilience
* **Cost:** 4 (causes Stun on Complication)
* **Duration:** Until broken
* **Category:** Curse spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster must know the target's true name and possess a token of them. On a success, the target's Complication range increases by +1 and one skill named by the caster at the time of casting is hindered: all tests using that skill increase in difficulty by +1. The curse is broken if the tablet is found and destroyed, or if a Rite of Cleansing is worked upon the victim.

**Flawed:** A flawed version of this spell cannot target a chosen skill; instead it hinders a skill chosen at random by the GM, which may not trouble the victim at all.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, hinder a second skill.
* For 3 Success Levels, increase the target's Complication range by a further +1.

## **Blessing of the Wild**

The wild places answer those who ask rightly, lending their swiftness and endurance to those they favour. Recipients move as though the forest itself parts before them, crossing broken ground, streams, and undergrowth with the ease of a stag in flight, their limbs untouched by fatigue.

**Traditions:** Celtic (the Gift of Arduinna), Germanic, Cult of Mormo

* **Skill:** Survival
* **Difficulty:** Hard
* **Cost:** 4 (causes Drain on Complication)
* **Duration:** A total number of rounds equal to the spellcaster's Pneuma rating, divided evenly among all affected targets (rounding all fractions down)
* **Category:** Blessing spell

**Spell Effect:** The spell may affect the spellcaster and any allies within Close range, up to a total number of targets equal to the spellcaster's Pneuma rating. For the duration of the spell, affected targets may move up to 1 additional zone whenever they move as a minor or major action, and may take one additional minor action for free each turn.

**Success Levels:** For every 2 Success Levels spent, the spellcaster or a single affected ally may take one additional major action during one of their own turns, provided that turn falls before the spell's duration ends. No character may gain more than one additional major action this way.

## **Blessing of Unnatural Vigour**

Power drawn from a corrupting, fecund source floods into the caster's allies, thickening their hides and knitting their wounds shut even as the wounds are dealt. It is a blessing that asks a price of the flesh: those it favours do not always come back entirely as they went in.

**Traditions:** Cult of Mormo (the Black Goat's Blessing), Cult of Cthulhu, Cult of Nyarlathotep

* **Skill:** Resilience
* **Difficulty:** Difficult
* **Cost:** 4 (causes Drain on Complication)
* **Duration:** Until the end of the current scene
* **Category:** Blessing spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster bestows this blessing upon their allies. Choose a number of allies equal to the spellcaster's Pneuma rating within Close range, increasing their Armour resistance by +2 and giving them the ability to regenerate 2 Stress at the end of each turn.

**Flawed:** A flawed version of this spell causes the recipients to mutate grotesquely, increasing their Complication range by 1.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, increase the Stress regeneration by an additional 2.
* For 1 Success Level, the spell affects one additional ally for each Success Level spent.

## **Bolt of the Heavens**

Winds rise and storm clouds gather as the caster draws down a bolt of raw heavenly force — a war-god's hammer, a Gaulish thunder-lord's axe, or the levin-bolt of a sky-father — to strike an enemy and scorch all who stand nearby. Whatever name is invoked, the sky itself answers the call.

**Traditions:** Germanic (the Hammer of Thunraz), Celtic (the Bolt of Taranis), Greco-Roman (the Keraunos)

* **Skill:** Fighting
* **Difficulty:** Difficult
* **Cost:** 4 (Piercing 1 on Complication)
* **Duration:** Instant
* **Category:** Attack spell

**Spell Effect:** The spell targets a single enemy or object within Medium range and inflicts Pneuma +2 physical Stress with the Area effect.

**Success Levels:**

* For 1 Success Level, replace the Area effect with Piercing 2.
* For 2 Success Levels, add the Vicious effect.
* For 2 Success Levels, add the Stun effect.

## **Call of the Deep**

The deep water calls, and something in the blood answers. Those who fall under this curse feel the pull of the nearest sea, lake, or river as an unbearable hunger, and will walk toward it heedless of blade, fire, or friend, until they slip beneath the surface and sink.

**Traditions:** Cult of Cthulhu, Cult of Nyarlathotep

* **Skill:** Resilience
* **Difficulty:** Challenging, opposed by the target's Willpower + Resilience
* **Cost:** 5 (causes Drain on Complication). In addition, each Effect rolled on the cost adds 1 to Doom.
* **Duration:** Until the end of the current scene
* **Category:** Curse spell

**Spell Effect:** This spell targets a single living creature within Medium range. If the target is affected, it moves toward the nearest large body of water it can see, at the GM's discretion as to whether a body of water is large enough. The only actions the target may take are moving toward that body of water and trying to break free of the curse, requiring a Willpower + Resilience test with a Challenging difficulty. If the target reaches the water, it enters and dives downward until it either breaks free of the curse or drowns. If the target cannot see a body of water, it may act normally, as if unaware of the curse, but the curse remains in effect until broken. The curse is broken when the target succeeds at the test to break free, when the caster is slain or rendered unconscious, or when a Rite of Cleansing or similar working is worked upon the target.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, the difficulty of any skill test to break free of the curse increases by 1. This is not repeatable.
* For 2 Success Levels, the target moves toward the nearest large body of water whether or not it can see it.

## **Call the Devourers**

Reaching through the thin veil that separates this world from a hungrier one, the caster calls forth mobs of the corpse-eating dead to fight at their side. They come obedient, after a fashion — but their appetite for demanding orders is greater than their patience, and a caster who asks too much of them courts disaster.

**Traditions:** Cult of Mormo (the Call the Corpse Eaters), Cult of Nyarlathotep

* **Skill:** Manipulation
* **Difficulty:** Routine, plus 1 for each ghoul mob
* **Cost:** 5 (Piercing 2 on Complication)
* **Duration:** Until the end of the current scene
* **Category:** Summoning spell

**Spell Effect:** The spell summons one, two, or three ghouls to aid the spellcaster. If multiple ghouls are summoned, they act as a mob. The ghouls obey the spellcaster's commands, though more demanding orders may require additional persuasion. The ghouls return to their realm when the spell ends.

**Flawed:** A flawed version of this spell summons the ghouls, but they are not under the spellcaster's control.

**Success Levels:**

* For 1 Success Level, summon one additional ghoul per Success Level spent.

## **Call the Storm**

Weather answers to the old bargains struck with the sky. A druid reads the wind through Ogham and calls it to heel; a Germanic seer chants Thunraz's name into gathering clouds; a Greek priest pours libations to Zeus Ombrios; a Deep One cultist calls the sea's own weather ashore. What comes is never quite tame.

**Traditions:** Celtic, Germanic, Greco-Roman, Cult of Cthulhu

* **Skill:** Survival
* **Difficulty:** Challenging
* **Cost:** 3 (causes Drain on Complication)
* **Duration:** The remainder of the scene
* **Category:** Manifestation spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster raises, calms, or reshapes wind and weather across the surrounding area for the rest of the scene: fog thick enough to hide a warband, wind to fill or empty sails, rain to douse fires and dampen powder-dry thatch, or a still and windless calm. This spell is not an attack and inflicts no Stress directly, though the GM may apply reasonable difficulty changes to actions the new weather would hinder or aid.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, the weather's effect covers a second adjacent zone or an area of similar size.
* For 2 Success Levels, the spellcaster may reshape the weather's precise character once more before the spell ends without recasting.

## **Conjure the Paredros**

A whispered invocation over incense, or three drops of wine poured to an unseen guest, calls up a paredros: a minor servitor spirit, part guardian angel and part errand-ghost, bound to the caster's will for a single working day. It carries no weapon and seeks no battle, but it goes where the caster cannot, and sees what the caster is not there to see.

**Traditions:** Greco-Roman, Hermetic, Coptic, Jewish Babylonian, Cult of Yog-Sothoth

* **Skill:** Manipulation
* **Difficulty:** Challenging
* **Cost:** 2
* **Duration:** The scene
* **Category:** Summoning spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster summons a minor servitor spirit that appears within Close range. The spirit is invisible to all but the caster, cannot fight or be harmed, and can fetch small objects, spy on a location within Medium range, carry a short message, or assist one ally's skill test per round by granting +1 die to that test, caster's choice each round. It vanishes at the end of the scene.

**Flawed:** A flawed version of this spell summons a paredros with a mind of its own: it performs the task asked of it, but adds one small, unhelpful complication of its own devising each time it is used, at the GM's discretion.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, the spirit may act twice in the same round, assisting two different tests.
* For 3 Success Levels, the spirit's duration extends to a full day and night rather than ending with the scene.

## **Consecrated Arms**

Blade, spear, and standard are given over to sacred purpose — anointed with oil, marked with a cross or a rune, breathed over with a magus's fire-prayer. Weapons so blessed bite true against the unnatural, where ordinary iron only glances off hide that should not exist.

**Traditions:** Christian, Mithraic, Zoroastrian, Germanic

* **Skill:** Fighting
* **Difficulty:** Challenging
* **Cost:** 3 (causes Drain on Complication)
* **Duration:** The remainder of the scene
* **Category:** Blessing spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster blesses a single weapon they or an ally within Close range carries. For the rest of the scene, attacks made with that weapon count as magical for the purpose of affecting supernatural creatures that require it, and gain the Piercing 1 damage effect.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, bless a second weapon within Close range with the same casting.
* For 2 Success Levels, the damage effect improves to Piercing 2 on all blessed weapons from this casting.

## **Corrosive Flesh**

The caster's own body is unmade and remade into something that eats away at whatever it touches, flesh gone slick and virulent. To close with such a caster in melee is to invite ruin: skin blisters and dissolves at the lightest contact, whether the caster means to strike or merely to be struck.

**Traditions:** Cult of Mormo (the Acidic Transformation), Cult of Yog-Sothoth

* **Skill:** Medicine
* **Difficulty:** Difficult
* **Cost:** 6 (Piercing 2 on Complication)
* **Duration:** A number of rounds equal to the spellcaster's Pneuma rating
* **Category:** Transmutation spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster transforms their own flesh into a corrosive substance, inflicting damage on those they touch. The spellcaster's unarmed attacks inflict +Pneuma physical Stress with the Persistent 4 and Piercing 1 damage effects. Enemies who make melee attacks or grapple the spellcaster with their natural weapons or unarmed attacks suffer 3 physical Stress with the Piercing 1 effect.

## **Swiftness of the Steed**

Calling on the swiftness of a legendary mount — an eight-legged steed of the Allfather, a chariot-horse of the sun, or the tireless stag of the hollow hills — the caster grants their companions a sudden, uncanny burst of speed, carrying them past danger before it can close.

**Traditions:** Germanic (the Swiftness of Sleipnir), Celtic, Greco-Roman

* **Skill:** Survival
* **Difficulty:** Difficult
* **Cost:** 3 (causes Drain on Complication)
* **Duration:** Instant
* **Category:** Blessing spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster and any allies within Close range may immediately move up to 2 zones. In addition, whenever an ally chooses to use Keep the Initiative before the spellcaster's next turn, it costs them 0 Success Levels to do so.

**Success Levels:** For 2 Success Levels, each affected character may take one additional minor action for free on their next turn.

## **Tongue of Beasts**

For the length of a scene, the babble of birds and beasts resolves into speech, and the caster can answer in kind. Cernunnos's votaries speak to the beasts of the wild wood as a right owed by kinship; Germanic seers ask it of raven and wolf, Wodan's own creatures; Zoroastrian magi treat it as recovering a fragment of the world's first, unbroken language. Mormo's cultists do not ask — they compel, and the beasts obey with a resentment that lingers after the spell ends.

**Traditions:** Celtic, Germanic, Zoroastrian, Cult of Mormo

* **Skill:** Survival
* **Difficulty:** Challenging
* **Cost:** 2 (causes Stun on Complication)
* **Duration:** The remainder of the scene
* **Category:** Blessing spell

**Spell Effect:** For the duration, the spellcaster can speak with and understand any natural animal within Close range as though it shared a common tongue. Animals answer according to their own nature and disposition — a spell effect cannot make a beast wiser, braver, or more loyal than it is.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, the spellcaster may ask a single willing service of an animal spoken to, within the bounds of what that creature could plausibly do.
* For 2 Success Levels, the range of the spell extends to Medium. Cult of Mormo casters who take this option instead compel one animal within range to obey a single order as though under a Control spell, whether it wishes to or not.

## **The Undying Flame**

Fed with clarified butter or sacred oil and never permitted to gutter, the consecrated flame stands sentinel over the camp through the night. It is Atar himself, the Zoroastrian holy fire, burning against the dark and the lie it harbours; even those who name it otherwise feel its warmth as something more than mere heat.

**Traditions:** Zoroastrian (signature), Mithraic, Hermetic

* **Skill:** Resilience
* **Difficulty:** Hard
* **Cost:** 4 (causes Drain on Complication)
* **Duration:** The scene, or until dawn if kindled overnight
* **Category:** Ward spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster consecrates a flame within their current zone, which must be kept burning for the duration. Any supernatural creature attempting to approach within Close range of the flame suffers Pneuma physical Stress with the Intense effect, resisted normally. Allies within Close range of the flame gain Courage resistance equal to the spellcaster's Pneuma rating.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, the warded radius extends to Medium range.
* For 2 Success Levels, the Stress inflicted on approaching supernatural creatures gains the Persistent 6 effect.

## **The Unfailing Vessel**

Oil that does not run dry, bread that multiplies past its measure, water made wholesome though it stood stagnant — the working turns want into sufficiency for a company that must march or wait another day. Christian ascetics know it from the widow's jar that never emptied; Irish druids call it the Cauldron's Plenty, after the Dagda's own vessel from which no one rose unsatisfied; Coptic monks bless the grain-store of a besieged village with the same rite.

**Traditions:** Christian, Celtic (the Cauldron's Plenty), Coptic, Germanic

* **Skill:** Medicine
* **Difficulty:** Challenging
* **Cost:** 2 (causes Drain on Complication)
* **Duration:** A single day
* **Category:** Manifestation spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster blesses a quantity of food, drink, or oil within Reach, sufficient to sustain a number of people equal to the spellcaster's Pneuma rating multiplied by four for the day, regardless of how little was present to begin with. The blessing also purifies spoiled or tainted provisions of the same quantity, rendering them wholesome and safe to consume.

**Flawed:** A flawed version of this spell is unreliable in its measure — roll 1d3 and halve the number of people sustained on a result of 1, though the purifying effect on spoiled provisions still functions fully.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, the duration extends to a full week if the provisions are kept and rationed with care.
* For 2 Success Levels, the number sustained doubles.

## **Veil of the Faceless**

The caster calls on an aspect of the Messenger that wears no true face, and their allies borrow that same inscrutability. Outlines shimmer and blur; blows that should land skid past flesh that is somehow never quite where the eye expects it. Hermetic philosophers who dare the working speak instead of a sphinx's riddle turned to armour, and the syncretic cults of the Nile know it as a mask worn by a god of a thousand names.

**Traditions:** Cult of Nyarlathotep (the Mask of the Faceless Sphinx), Hermetic, Cult of Yog-Sothoth, Coptic

* **Skill:** Academia
* **Difficulty:** Challenging
* **Cost:** 4 (causes Persistent Drain on Complication)
* **Duration:** A number of rounds equal to the spellcaster's Pneuma rating
* **Category:** Blessing spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster and any allies within Close range when the spell is cast become harder to hit, increasing the difficulty of attacks against them by +1. Further, they count as having heavy cover.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, the difficulty increase for attacks against the affected characters increases to +2.
* For 2 Success Levels, affected characters receive +4 Cover resistance instead of the +2 Cover resistance normally granted by heavy cover.
* For 3 Success Levels, any ranged or melee attack that misses an affected character inflicts 4 mental damage with the Stun damage effect upon the attacker.

## **Visions of Dread**

The mind is shown what it was never meant to see: giants and monsters from the icy wastes, the dreaming god beneath the waves, or the cold void where mad Azathoth gnaws at the centre of all things. Whatever a tradition's private horror, those touched by this working are struck dumb with terror, their courage curdling into confusion and dread.

**Traditions:** Germanic (the Curse of Loki), Cult of Cthulhu, Cult of Yog-Sothoth (the Gaze of the Multiverse), Cult of Mormo

* **Skill:** Manipulation
* **Difficulty:** Difficult
* **Cost:** 5 (generates Doom on Complication)
* **Duration:** Instant
* **Category:** Attack spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster inflicts Pneuma +2 mental Stress with the Stun effect on all enemies within Close range.

**Flawed:** A flawed version of this spell affects all creatures within range, not just enemies.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, add the Persistent 6 effect to the Stress.
* For 2 Success Levels, add the Snare effect to the Stress.
* For 2 Success Levels, add the Piercing 1 effect to the Stress.

## **Voice of Command**

A word, or a few words, spoken with the full weight of the caster's will behind them, and the listener's own will simply gives way. Flee. Kneel. Drop it. Sleep. Greek goētes call it the compelling voice; Coptic scribes bind it into the names of angels who do not suffer argument; Hermetic philosophers name it a working upon the hegemonikon, the ruling faculty of the mind. Nyarlathotep's servants use it more cruelly, relishing the moment a victim's resistance breaks.

**Traditions:** Greco-Roman, Hermetic, Coptic, Cult of Nyarlathotep

* **Skill:** Manipulation
* **Difficulty:** Difficult, opposed by the target's Willpower + Resilience
* **Cost:** 4 (causes Stun on Complication)
* **Duration:** Instant
* **Category:** Control spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster speaks a single short command — flee, kneel, drop your weapon, sleep, or similar — at one mortal target within Close range. If the target loses the opposed test, they suffer Pneuma mental Stress with the Stun effect and must obey the command to the best of their ability on their next turn. If this Stress reduces the target's Psychē to its last point, they instead fall fully under the spellcaster's control for the remainder of the scene, obeying any command that does not obviously destroy them.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, the command may be up to a full sentence rather than a few words.
* For 2 Success Levels, the range increases to Medium.

## **Weaving of Fate**

The threads of destiny are not fixed for those who know how to plead with the powers that spin them — three sparing sisters, the Norns beneath the World Tree, or fate itself. A mortal's plea, offered with due deference, may yet turn aside a stroke of ill or ill-luck fortune, though the powers exact a balance in return.

**Traditions:** Greco-Roman (invoking the Parcae), Germanic (a plea to the Norns), Celtic, Hermetic

* **Skill:** Manipulation
* **Difficulty:** Very Hard
* **Cost:** 4 (causes Stun on Complication)
* **Duration:** Instant
* **Category:** Blessing or curse spell

**Spell Effect:** This spell allows the spellcaster to plea to the powers of fate to modify their own fate or that of another. As a reaction to any skill test performed within Close range — whether made by friend or foe — the spellcaster may attempt this plea, and their player chooses one of the following effects:

* **Critical Success:** Change the result of a single d12 from the skill test to 1, generating two successes automatically.
* **Complication:** Change the result of a single d12 from the skill test to its maximum value, generating a complication.
* **Re-Roll:** Force the ally or enemy to re-roll all dice from the skill test before determining the outcome.

After the spellcaster casts this spell, the target gains the scar **The Fates Must Be Balanced** until the spellcaster rolls a complication on a skill test. While this scar persists, the target's complication range on all skill tests increases by +1.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, choose two of this spell's effects when invoking fate.
* For 2 Success Levels, the spellcaster may react to skill tests performed within Medium range.
* For 3 Success Levels, the spellcaster may react to skill tests performed within Long range.

## **Word of Peace**

A calm word, a psalm, a struck lyre-chord, and the madness of battle-fury or terror loosens its grip. Christian ascetics speak it as intercession against despair; Greek musicians call it the Orphic Chord, the same music said to have charmed the beasts of the underworld into stillness; Coptic monks chant it over the possessed and the raving alike.

**Traditions:** Christian, Coptic, Greco-Roman (the Orphic Chord), Celtic

* **Skill:** Manipulation
* **Difficulty:** Challenging
* **Cost:** 3 (causes Stun on Complication)
* **Duration:** Instant
* **Category:** Blessing spell

**Spell Effect:** The spellcaster ends any ongoing Frightened condition or frenzy effect currently affecting any number of allies within Close range, and each affected ally immediately removes mental Stress equal to the spellcaster's Pneuma rating.

**Flawed:** A flawed version of this spell cannot distinguish friend from foe: it calms all creatures within Close range, allies and enemies alike, which can be disastrous mid-battle.

**Success Levels:**

* For 2 Success Levels, the spell also removes one mental injury from each affected ally.
* For 2 Success Levels, the spell's range extends to Medium.
