# NPC complication integration

Status: Approved shared-rule additions to the working procedure in [Complication Die Resolution](complication-die-resolution.md). These decisions await coordinated integration into the published rules. This addendum does not change published ability entries.

## Power mitigation and supplementary declarations

Reserve Effect Power and declare Power mitigation before supplementary declarations close. If a reaction such as Scan cancels a purchase, its Power stays spent; the cancellation does not reopen declarations or free Power for mitigation.

## Redirection and reduction

Cancelling or mitigating a complication is separate from redirecting or reducing an imposed status. Interpose redirects only an eligible status, not the complication that caused it or any separate consequence. Hold the Line reduces only the ordinary Disposition loss permitted by its rule, not complication severity.

These abilities grant no new permission for a complication to inflict a status or additional Disposition loss.
