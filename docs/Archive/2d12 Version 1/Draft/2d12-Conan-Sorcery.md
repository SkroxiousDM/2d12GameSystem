# 2d12 Sorcery Chapter

## Cosmic Sorcery

### The Time Is Right
- **Skill:** Knowledge (arcana).
- **Difficulty:** Hard.
- **Cost:** 1 mental stress.
- **Duration:** Instant.
- **Category:** Manifestation.
- **Spell Effect:** When you cast your next battlefield spell this scene, you may treat the casting roll as if you had rolled a 20 for purposes of the final result. This does not change the spell’s Cost.
- **Flawed:** A flawed version grants the benefit only if the next spell is a battlefield spell cast before the end of the current scene.
- **Success Levels:** Spend 2 Success Levels to also gain +1 Morale resistance against the next spell’s Cost.

### The Stars Are Right
- **Skill:** Knowledge (arcana).
- **Difficulty:** Very Hard.
- **Cost:** 2 mental stress.
- **Duration:** Instant.
- **Category:** Manifestation.
- **Spell Effect:** When you cast your next battlefield spell or ritual while this spell remains relevant to the same target or situation, you may treat the casting roll as if you had rolled a 20. In addition, the target’s resistance against that spell is reduced by 1 for the purpose of the casting test.
- **Flawed:** The resistance reduction does not apply.
- **Success Levels:** Spend 2 Success Levels to extend the duration of the spell’s preparation effect until the end of the next scene.

### Alignment of the Heavenly Paths
- **Skill:** Knowledge (arcana).
- **Difficulty:** Difficult.
- **Cost:** 2 mental stress.
- **Duration:** Instant.
- **Category:** Manifestation.
- **Spell Effect:** You align favorable cosmic influences around a single spell you cast immediately after this one. That spell gains +1 bonus on its casting test, or +2 if the target or subject is directly connected to fate, prophecy, or celestial omens.
- **Flawed:** The bonus is always only +1.
- **Success Levels:** Spend 1 Success Level to reduce the next spell’s Cost by 1, to a minimum of 0.

### Horoscope
- **Skill:** Knowledge (arcana).
- **Difficulty:** Routine.
- **Cost:** 1 mental stress.
- **Duration:** Scene.
- **Category:** Divination.
- **Spell Effect:** Ask one question about a target’s immediate fate, likely course of action, or the best time to act. The answer should be brief and useful, not absolute.
- **Flawed:** The answer is less precise and may include an omen or symbolic image instead of a direct statement.
- **Success Levels:** For each Success Level spent, ask one additional question.

### The Hour of Your Birth
- **Skill:** Knowledge (arcana).
- **Difficulty:** Challenging.
- **Cost:** 1 mental stress.
- **Duration:** Instant.
- **Category:** Divination.
- **Spell Effect:** Learn one significant fact about the target’s destiny, such as a vulnerability, a defining trait, or a future danger tied to their birth omen.
- **Flawed:** You learn only a symbolic clue.
- **Success Levels:** Spend 1 Success Level to learn a second fact.

### The Hour of Your Death
- **Skill:** Knowledge (arcana).
- **Difficulty:** Difficult.
- **Cost:** 2 mental stress.
- **Duration:** Instant.
- **Category:** Divination.
- **Spell Effect:** Learn one likely circumstance of the target’s death, framed as a prophecy rather than a guarantee.
- **Flawed:** The prophecy is vague or metaphorical.
- **Success Levels:** Spend 2 Success Levels to ask one extra question about how to avoid, hasten, or redirect that fate.

### Dictate Fate
- **Skill:** Knowledge (arcana).
- **Difficulty:** Hard.
- **Cost:** 2 mental stress.
- **Duration:** Special.
- **Category:** Divination.
- **Spell Effect:** Impose a foreshadowing on a target you can meaningfully identify. Later, when the target is in a situation relevant to that foreshadowing, the GM should make that fate matter in play through a strong coincidence, opportunity, or danger.
- **Flawed:** The foreshadowing is weaker and only appears once.
- **Success Levels:** Spend 2 Success Levels to make the foretold event harder for the target to avoid.

### Nothing Is Immutable
- **Skill:** Knowledge (arcana).
- **Difficulty:** Very Hard.
- **Cost:** 3 mental stress.
- **Duration:** Permanent.
- **Category:** Transmutation.
- **Spell Effect:** Transform one mundane object into another mundane object of roughly the same size and general mass. If the target is living, it resists with a suitable opposed test at the GM’s discretion.
- **Flawed:** The transformation is unstable and can be undone by a strong opposing magical effect.
- **Success Levels:** Spend 2 Success Levels to change the object into something one size category larger or smaller, within reason.

### Make Gate
- **Skill:** Knowledge (arcana).
- **Difficulty:** Very Hard.
- **Cost:** 4 mental stress.
- **Duration:** Scene.
- **Category:** Summoning.
- **Spell Effect:** Create a functioning magical gate between two prepared points or two known locations. The gate remains open only while the duration lasts and must be anchored by suitable symbols, runes, or ritual marks.
- **Flawed:** The gate is unstable; the first creature or object through it may trigger a complication.
- **Success Levels:** Spend 2 Success Levels to extend duration to the end of the next scene.

## Counterspells

### Blessing of True Steel
- **Skill:** Knowledge (arcana).
- **Difficulty:** Routine.
- **Cost:** 1 mental stress.
- **Duration:** Scene.
- **Category:** Blessing, Ward.
- **Spell Effect:** Enchant one metal weapon. While the spell lasts, the wielder gains +1 bonus on tests to resist spells and can disrupt one ongoing spell effect on a successful hit if the target is visibly under magical influence.
- **Flawed:** The spell only grants the resistance bonus.
- **Success Levels:** Spend 2 Success Levels to increase the resistance bonus to +2.

### Master Warding

- **Duration:** Scene.

- **Flawed:** The ward only resists hostile magic cast directly at you.
- **Success Levels:** Spend 2 Success Levels to extend the ward to a small area around you instead of just your person.
