Twenty Focuses steeped in the Frontier: afflicted blood, bounties, relics and ruins, the old faith, villages holding the line until dawn, and the people who love what they should hate.

> Dhampirs enter play here, through Carries Her Mother's Blood.

## 1. Focus Rules

Focus rules are unchanged from *Generic Focuses* §1:

| Element | Detail |
|----|----|
| **Power tags** | 2 |
| **Weakness tag** | 1 |
| **PC link** | A prompt that connects you to another PC |
| **Tier 1 ability** | Fixed; gained at creation for free |
| **Tier 2 ability** | Choose 1 of 2; after 3 Advances, costs an Advance |
| **Tier 3 ability** | Choose 1 of 2; after 6 Advances, costs an Advance. Exactly one option is ★ Major (1 Refresh) |

- **◈ Weird** Focuses assume afflicted blood, old-world works, or the supernatural. In this setting all are allowed; the marker shows which ones draw attention from vampire households, Occultists, and frightened villagers.

- **Generic Focuses:** the 24 Focuses in *Generic Focuses* also work here. Good fits are *Never Leaves Anyone Behind*, *Masters a Weapon*, *Moves Like a Shadow*, *Never Misses*, *Opens Every Lock*, *Talks Their Way Out of Anything*, *Lives Off the Land*, and *Made a Bargain With Something Old*. Use the setting Focuses below in place of *Hunts the Things in the Dark* and *Is More Than Human*.

- **Shared reminders:** free bonus dice count toward the +2 maximum; only one cost reduction applies to a single Effect; abilities that add bonus above the tag cap are limited to **+1 above the normal +2 cap (maximum +3)** unless stated otherwise (Generic Focuses §9.2).

## 3. Bloodline Focuses

### 1. Carries Her Mother's Blood ◈

> Dhampir. Born to one of the Kept, and carrying a little of what she is. Tougher than you should be, slower to age, never easy in the sun — and worth more to certain people than you'd like.

- **Power tags:** *stronger than I look*, *eyes that see in the dark*

- **Weakness tag:** *my mother's blood* (when recognised, the scene gains *Feared and Despised* among common folk, **or** draws the interest of a vampire household that wants you as an heir, a key or something worse; GM's choice, compel normally)

- **PC link:** "\[PC\] knows who my mother was. I've never asked how."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Mother's Blood** | You notice fresh blood anywhere in the scene, and by scent or taste you can tell the blood of the afflicted from ordinary blood. A short rest restores **2 + Edge** Sōma (not in direct sunlight). Some old-world doors and instruments respond to you: when you try to make one work, *my mother's blood* counts as a tag, and works best on things tied to her line. You're subject to **Sunlight Syndrome** and **the Thirst** (Frontier Rules §7). |
| 2a | **Dhampir Regeneration** | Once per scene, lower one physical status by **2 tiers** (no roll). Not in direct sunlight. Thirst +1. |
| 2b | **Read the Blood** | Touch or taste a blood sample (a scene, no roll) to learn: whether it is afflicted, a dhampir's, or ordinary; whether the afflicted person it came from was well fed or starving; and roughly how long ago it was shed. With **1 Power** from a Discover effect, also learn whether two afflicted samples share a bloodline. Thirst +1 each time. |
| 3a | **Mother's Key** | Old-world systems tied to your mother's line accept you as one of her line. **Once per session**, make one such system open, start, stop or show its records — no roll. On systems of other lines, *my mother's blood* gives a free bonus die. The GM may spend **2 Woe** to have the system note your visit somewhere someone will notice. |
| 3b | **★ Awaken the Blood** | Once per scene, let your mother's blood rise (*Thirst*): until the scene ends, you get **+2 bonus dice** free on physical rolls, your Attacks inflict **+1 tier**, and your physical statuses can't go above tier 5. Afterwards, Thirst +2, and anyone who saw it treats you as one of the Kept. |

**Optional swap for combat-focused tables:** replace 3a with:

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 3a (alt) | **Knows How They Die** | Your Attacks against the afflicted inflict **+1 tier** when they are *Sun-Sick*, *starving* or *broken*, and your blade counts as beheading (a killing method) against an afflicted target with a tier-4+ physical status. |

**Table options for dhampirs (choose at session zero):**

- **Rare blood** (recommended default): only one PC in the party can take this Focus.

- **Costly blood:** this Focus costs **1 Refresh** at creation.

- **The sun is merciless:** Sun-Sick gains +2 per scene instead of +1 (Frontier Rules §7.1).

- **Known mother:** your mother is alive and identified at creation. She is an NPC with her own household and agenda. Gain a free bonus die on your first Mother's Key or Read the Blood roll involving her line each session; the GM gains 1 Woe at the start of each session she is involved.

- **Unknown mother:** you don't know who she was. Finding out is a Goal; the GM secretly decides her line.

### 2. Was Kept and Got Out ◈

> You were a donor in a vampire household — by birth, debt or choice. You got out. They remember you.

- **Power tags:** *I know how households run*, *I know how they think*

- **Weakness tag:** *they'll want me back*

- **PC link:** "\[PC\] got me out. I've never told them what it cost."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Household Ways** | *Bled* gains **1 fewer tier** on you. You get a free bonus die to Notice or Empathy when judging a vampire's hunger or a household's customs. |
| 2a | **Old Ties** | Once per session, a retainer or donor still loyal to you gives a Discover answer or a small favour. The household can find you the same way (the GM spends 1 Woe). |
| 2b | **One of Them, Almost** | Retainers and donors take you for one of their own. Deceive rolls to pass among them are **two steps easier**. |
| 3a | **Unbroken** | When you Resist Presence, intimidation or fear from a vampire with Pneuma, reduce the status by **3 tiers**, and the cost is 0 on a highest die of 5+. |
| 3b | **★ Turn the Bond** | Once per session, in a Duel of Wills against your former household, gain a free bonus die on every roll. If you win, you also learn where it keeps its blood. |

## 4. Hunter Focuses

### 3. Hunts the Kept

> Your trade is the hardest one on the Frontier: you kill vampires.

- **Power tags:** *knows where they hide*, *a stake for every heart*

- **Weakness tag:** *the Kept know my name*

- **PC link:** "\[PC\] was there for my first vampire kill. It went badly."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Know Your Quarry** | Once per scene, Discover one of a vampire's weakness tags without rolling. |
| 2a | **Killing Mastery** | Your killing strikes (Frontier Rules §5.2) are **Standard 15** instead of Hard 18. |
| 2b | **Steel Against Presence** | When you Resist a vampire's Presence or intimidation with Pneuma, the cost is **0 on a highest die of 5+**. |
| 3a | **Find the Killing Way** | Upgrading a vampire's weakness tag into a killing method (Frontier Rules §8.1) costs **2 Power** instead of 3. |
| 3b | **★ The Hunter's Mark** | Once per scene, mark a vampire you can see. Until the scene ends, all allies' Attacks against it inflict **+1 tier**, and it can't use *The Old Door* to escape. |

### 4. Collects the Bounty

> A Hunter is a professional. You take the job, you finish it, and you get paid.

- **Power tags:** *always gets paid*, *proof in a sack*

- **Weakness tag:** *money first*

- **PC link:** "\[PC\] owes me their share of the last bounty."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Professional** | At the start of each contract, the party gains **+1 Momentum**. Once per contract, ask a free Discover: "What isn't the client telling us?" |
| 2a | **Beat the Competition** | When rival Hunters race you for a bounty (Frontier Rules §10.4), their project clock ticks **1 less** each World Turn, and you gain +1 Advance on any roll that gets ahead of them. |
| 2b | **Bring Them In Alive** | Your Attacks can inflict *subdued* instead of harm. A target Taken Out by *subdued* is captured, and a living quarry is worth **one step more** bounty. |
| 3a | **My Reputation Precedes Me** | Your reputation tag (Frontier Rules §10.3) counts as a free extra tag in Negotiation, adding up to **+1 above the normal +2 cap (maximum +3 from tags)**. Clients always pay half up front. |
| 3b | **★ The Big Score** | Once per session, when you complete a contract, choose one extra reward: an artifact from the quarry's hoard, a Region Truth in your favour, or a second *vampire's bounty*. |

### 5. Hunts the Beasts of the Frontier

- **Power tags:** *knows every monster*, *nets, poison, and patience*

- **Weakness tag:** *chases the trophy*

- **PC link:** "\[PC\] pulled me out of a beast's jaws. I still have the tooth."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Beast Lore** | Once per scene, Discover a monster's weakness tag (not a vampire's) without rolling. |
| 2a | **Baited Trap** | Spend 1 Power to Create *Baited Trap*. A beast that enters it is *Impeded* until it breaks free (an Athletics roll vs. Hard 18). |
| 2b | **Harvest** | After killing a monster, spend a scene to harvest it for one cypher (tier 2–3) that matches its power: venom, hide, a gland, an eye. |
| 3a | **Bane of Beasts** | Your Attacks against monsters inflict **+1 tier**. |
| 3b | **★ Monster-Slayer** | In Fights against monsters (not vampires), the enemy's Disposition starts **−3**, and minion beasts flee after your first successful Attack. |

- **Weakness tag:** *I'll sacrifice anything for this*

- **PC link:** "\[PC\] knows who I'm hunting, and promised to help me."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | **The One** | Name your vampire. You always know when you're within a day's ride of it, and once per session ask a free Discover about it. |
| 2a | **Cold Fury** | When you Resist the named vampire's Presence, intimidation or fear, the cost is **0**. |
| 2b | **Nothing Held Back** | When you burn a tag against the named vampire or its servants, gain **+2 Power** instead of +1. |
| 3a | **Its Servants Die First** | The named vampire's servants and retainers start conflicts against you with **−2 Disposition**. |
| 3b | **★ This Ends Tonight** | When you face the named vampire, gain a free bonus die on every roll against it, and your killing strikes are **Standard 15**. If it escapes you, gain a Pneuma Scar. |

- **Weakness tag:** *the Kept will want it back*

- **PC link:** "\[PC\] knows how I got the relic. It isn't a good story."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Relic Bearer** | Start with one old-world artifact (Frontier Rules §9.2, or design one with the GM). It costs **no attunement Refresh**. |
| 2a | **Deeper Power** | Your relic gains a **second ability** (designed with the GM). |
| 2b | **Well-Kept** | Your relic's depletion improves by 1 (1 in d12 becomes no depletion). |
| 3a | **Relic Sense** | You sense other old-world artifacts within a day's walk, and once per scene ask a free Discover about one you can see. |
| 3b | **★ The Relic Awakens** | Your relic's tag may add **+1 above the normal +2 tag cap (maximum +3)**. Once per session, it acts by itself: one automatic success with **3 Power** spent on its listed Effects. |

### 8. Plunders the Old Estates

> Every abandoned estate holds treasure, machines, and traps. You go in for the first, use the second, and survive the third.

- **Power tags:** *knows old-world treasure*, *quick hands in dark halls*

- **Weakness tag:** *can't leave treasure behind*

- **PC link:** "\[PC\] and I came out of the same estate. Only one of us came out with the loot."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Salvager** | In each estate, lab, or ruin you search, find one old-world cypher (roll on Frontier Rules §9.1) without spending Power. |
| 2a | **Appraiser** | You know an artifact's tags, ability, depletion, and quirk as soon as you handle it. |
| 2b | **Tomb Sense** | When you enter a room, the GM reveals any trap Truths in it. When you Resist traps, your Edge counts as 1 higher. |
| 3a | **Cypher Hoarder** | Your Cypher Limit is **+1**. |
| 3b | **★ Thief of the Night** | You may attune one old-world artifact at no Refresh cost, and once per session, take an item from a vampire's presence without a roll, as long as the vampire isn't actively watching it. |

### 9. Carries a Passenger ◈

> A voice rides with you, through a relic at your ear, a grille in a buckle, a speaker no bigger than a coin. It speaks from an old-world site somewhere behind you: a shrine, an archive, a buried hospital. It says it was a person before the fall. It is rude, curious and out of date, and it has saved your life more than once.

- **Power tags:** *a voice that knows things*, *the old works listen to it*

- **Weakness tag:** *it has its own plans*

- **PC link:** "\[PC\] is the only one the Passenger likes. That worries me."

- **Home site:** choose it with the GM at creation. The Passenger can't leave it; only its voice travels.

- **Under open sky:** out of range, the Passenger can still reach you through the moving lights (Frontier Rules §9.3). At the start of a scene under open sky, roll a d12: on **9+** one is overhead, and the Passenger can speak and use its abilities for that scene. Each time it speaks this way, the GM may compel *someone else heard* or spend 1 Woe to have the message overheard.

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | **The Passenger** | Once per scene, ask a free Discover: the Passenger answers, truthfully but not always helpfully, and often out of date. It can talk to others through the relic, which is often a compel. **Range:** it speaks only within reach of its home site's relays, by default about a day's ride. Out of range it goes quiet and this Focus's abilities don't work. A working old-world relay (a lit terminal, a speaker grille, a site where the lights are still on) lets it speak for that scene; finding or waking one may take a Science roll. |
| 2a | **Override** | Once per scene, the Passenger takes hold of an old-world effect it can reach (a cypher, a guardian, a door, the lights, a trap or machine): **Disrupt** it for 0 Power. The GM may compel its curiosity afterwards. |
| 2b | **Talk You Through It** | In the Players' Turn, spend 1 Check to bring it something it wants (a record, news of the present, a part for its site). Next session, once per scene, it talks you through field treatment: lower one of your physical statuses by 2 tiers. |
| 3a | **Older Than the Kept** | Once per scene, take a free bonus die on an Old Lore or Occult roll. The Passenger remembers the age before the fall, or its records do. |
| 3b | **★ Do Exactly What I Say** | Once per session, follow the Passenger's instructions exactly for one action: it succeeds automatically with **4 Power**. Then the GM names the price it demands (a record, a favour, a secret, a visit to its home site, a scene where you do only what it says). |

## 6. Occult Focuses

### 10. Sends the Mind Abroad ◈

> Your mind can leave your body and walk where you can't: through estate walls, into dreams, across the desert.

- **Power tags:** *my mind walks free*, *dreamsight*

- **Weakness tag:** *my body lies helpless*

- **PC link:** "\[PC\] watches over my body when I go walking. I trust no one else."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Astral Self** | Leave your body as an unseen astral form (range about 1 km). You use Occult for Stealth and Notice. Your body is helpless and *Impeded* while you're out. Returning costs 1 Pneuma. |
| 2a | **Astral Strike** | Your astral form can Attack with Occult, inflicting mental statuses (*terrified*, *dazed*) even on vampires. |
| 2b | **Dream Walker** | Enter a sleeper's dream, including a bled victim's. Ask 2 free Discover questions about what they saw, or Restore their fear statuses. |
| 3a | **Far-Walker** | Your astral range becomes a day's ride, and you can bring one willing ally's mind with you. |
| 3b | **★ Astral Avatar** | Once per scene, your astral form becomes solid enough to Fight. It uses your skills, and harm to it comes out of your **Pneuma** pool, not physical statuses. |

### 11. Keeps the Old Faith

> Crosses don't burn anything, whatever the stories say. But the rites are older than anyone can explain, the faithful stand together, and some vampires still believe. You keep the old ways anyway.

- **Power tags:** *the faithful follow me*, *prayer steadies me*

- **Weakness tag:** *bound by the old laws* (you can't refuse sanctuary or lie under oath)

- **PC link:** "\[PC\] doesn't believe, but they carry the cross I gave them."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | **Holy Symbol** | Your cross creates *Warded* for 1 Power against any vampire who *believes the old stories* (Frontier Rules §8.1). Against other vampires it does nothing, but you and allies who can see it gain +1 Pneuma Edge against fear. |
| 2a | **Consecrate** | In a scene of prayer, bless water or blades. The old rites call for silver in the water: blessed water and blades count as *silvered* (Frontier Rules §8.1) for the mission. |
| 2b | **Last Rites** | Once per scene, every ally who can hear your prayer restores 2 Pneuma, and Resists fear with Edge +1 until the scene ends. |
| 3a | **Faith Unbroken** | When you Resist fear, Presence or despair with Pneuma, the cost is **0 on a highest die of 5+**. |
| 3b | **★ Holy Ground** | Once per session, sanctify a place: create the Truth *Holy Ground* for the scene. Believers can't enter without defying a compel, and every vampire Woe spend there costs **+1** while the faithful stand together. |

## 10. Design Notes

- **Dhampir as a Focus** lets any playbook carry afflicted blood: a dhampir Sellsword is the classic Hunter, but a dhampir Frontier Doctor or Broker plays very differently. The dhampir's edge is no longer raw power but access and information: reading blood, opening doors tied to her mother's line, and being wanted by people who matter. Sunlight Syndrome and the Thirst remain the price. *Knows How They Die* is offered for tables that want a fighter. Use the table options if it outshines other Focuses.

- **Overlaps with playbooks:** *Heals the Bled* stacks with the Frontier Doctor's *Treat the Bled*, and *Holds the Line Until Dawn* stacks with the Warden. Both stacks are intended specialist builds; none of them reduce the same Power cost twice.

- **Vampire-specific Focuses** (*Hunts the Kept*, *Seeks Revenge on a Vampire*) rely on killing strikes. If a campaign has few vampires, steer players toward *Hunts the Beasts of the Frontier* or *Collects the Bounty*.

- **Carries a Passenger** is an Old-World Focus. The Passenger is a voice relayed from a fixed old-world site, so it has a range; out of range the Focus does nothing except under open sky, where the moving lights give it a 1-in-3 chance each scene. On long journeys, place working relays often enough that the player isn't left without their Focus for whole sessions.

- **★ options** are all at Tier 3, as in *Generic Focuses*, so a character can always choose a path that keeps their Refresh.

- **Tag-cap exceptions** (Bears an Old-World Relic — The Relic Awakens; Forges Weapons Against the Night — Masterwork): aligned with the **Generic Focuses §9.2** fix — both now add **+1 above the normal +2 cap (maximum +3)** instead of ignoring it outright.
