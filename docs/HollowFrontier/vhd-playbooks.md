### 2.4 Handout — The Hunters' Field Guide

Hunters pass these notes from hand to hand. Give them to players as found knowledge. The guide is right about what hunters saw. Its explanations are another matter. The GM measures a vampire by threat rank (Frontier Rules §5.1), not by these classes.

**The Kinds of the Kept, as Hunters Tell It**

**Winterers.** The oldest and the worst. They go to ground for a generation and wake hungry enough to empty a village. Cunning, patient, and they know every family in the valley. *Hunters say:* the old ones sleep by choice, to outlast their enemies. Find the sleeping place and burn it.

**Strays.** Newly made, and it shows: clumsy, greedy, careless with their kills. They seldom last more than a few years. *Hunters say:* the blood thins with every making, and a made one is a weak copy of its maker. Kill them young and you'll have little trouble.

**Tapsters.** They take a little and leave you living, the way a tapster draws from a cask. Their donors wake dazed and remember nothing, and many go back willingly. They rarely kill. *Hunters say:* their bite carries a sleeping venom. Never let one near your neck, and never trust a town that seems glad of them.

## 10. Design Notes

### 10.1 Balance Targets

| Playbook | Main pool | Conflict role | Exploration role | Social role |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| Sellsword | Sōma | Attack / Defend, killing strikes | Forcing obstacles | Intimidation |
| Stalker | Akmē | Feint / Maneuver | Estates by day, security | Deception |
| Frontier Doctor | Psychē | Maneuver (Discover), Restore | Investigation, healing, the bled | Expertise, trust |
| Broker | Pneuma | Negotiation and Argument lead | Rumours, contacts | Persuasion, contracts |
| Rider | Sōma/Akmē | Ranged Attack, Chase, Hunt | Travel, Grind, tracking | Local knowledge |
| Occultist | Pneuma/Psychē | Flexible (Domain), Duel of Wills | Supernatural and old-world access | Varies by Domain |
| Mutant | Sōma/Akmē | Attack / Maneuver | Climbing, hiding, wastelands | Monsters and servants |
| Cyborg Hunter | Sōma | Ranged Attack, heavy firepower | Old-world machines, estates | Intimidation |
| Warden | Sōma/Psychē | Siege of the Night, Defend | Preparation, fortification | Villagers, local law |

### 10.2 Things to Watch in Playtest

- **Mutant and Cyborg core features** each grant two tagged abilities for free, more than other playbooks. They're balanced by *Feared and Despised* (Mutant) and glitches and a low Pneuma pool (Cyborg). If they outshine others, drop to 1 free Mutation or Implant.

- **Hard to Kill vs. More Machine Than Man:** the Cyborg capstone is strong by design at 12 Advances. Watch that it doesn't end the threat of mundane harm entirely.

- **Warden** is strongest in sieges and weaker on estate raids. Make sure the campaign has both, or let the Warden's traps travel (Trapmaker works anywhere).

- **Free bonus dice** (Stake Driver, Horse-Bond, Targeting eye, Combat Mode, Full Transformation) still count toward the +2 bonus die maximum.

- **★ count:** each playbook has two Major abilities and a free capstone, as in *Generic Playbooks*.
