Twenty adjectives for people hardened by the Frontier: the Bitten, the Coveted, the Frontier-Hard, the Vengeful. Each comes with a Frontier hook, one question about your past that often becomes your first Belief.

> The adjective of the character sentence.

## 1. Descriptor Rules

Each Descriptor gives (Core Rules §3.1):

| Element                | Detail                                |
|------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| **Pool bonus**         | +2 to one pool                        |
| **Power tag**          | 1                                     |
| **Weakness tag**       | 1 (can be compelled for Tychē Points) |
| **Suggested Instinct** | Use it or write your own              |

Each entry also has a **Frontier hook**: one question to answer at character creation. The answer often becomes your first Belief or a relationship.

## 3. Descriptors

### 1. Bitten

> A vampire drank from you once and let you live. The marks never faded.

- **Pool:** +2 Pneuma

- **Power tag:** *I've been bled and lived*

- **Weakness tag:** *marks on my throat*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always keep my collar buttoned."

- **Frontier hook:** Which vampire bit you, and why did it stop?

### 2. Bitter

> The Frontier taught you that hope is how people get killed.

- **Pool:** +2 Psychē

- **Power tag:** *expects the worst*

- **Weakness tag:** *trusts no one*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always ask who benefits."

- **Frontier hook:** Who betrayed you, and do they still live?

### 3. City-Born

> You grew up behind the city's walls, with books, machines, and safety. The Frontier is a different world.

- **Pool:** +2 Psychē

- **Power tag:** *educated in the city*

- **Weakness tag:** *soft hands*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always look for the scientific explanation first."

- **Frontier hook:** Why did you leave the city, and can you ever go back?

### 4. Charming

> People like you. Even the ones who shouldn't.

- **Pool:** +2 Pneuma

- **Power tag:** *a smile that opens doors*

- **Weakness tag:** *can't resist a pretty face*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always buy the first round."

- **Frontier hook:** Who in the last town is still waiting for you to come back?

### 5. Cold

> You learned to feel nothing. It keeps you alive.

- **Pool:** +2 Pneuma

- **Power tag:** *nothing shows on my face*

- **Weakness tag:** *pushes people away*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Never say goodbye."

- **Frontier hook:** What was the last thing that made you weep?

### 6. Coveted

> You have the kind of beauty the Kept collect: as brides, as servants, as trophies.

- **Pool:** +2 Pneuma

- **Power tag:** *beauty the vampires notice*

- **Weakness tag:** *the Kept want me*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always cover my face when I travel at night."

- **Frontier hook:** Which vampire household has already sent for you?

### 7. Cunning

> You win by thinking. Fights are for people who planned badly.

- **Pool:** +2 Psychē

- **Power tag:** *always a second plan*

- **Weakness tag:** *too clever by half*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always know the way out before I go in."

- **Frontier hook:** What scheme of yours went wrong, and who paid for it?

### 8. Devout

> The old faith is nearly gone, but you still keep it. Crosses don't burn anyone, but the rites still matter.

- **Pool:** +2 Pneuma

- **Power tag:** *faith the dark can feel*

- **Weakness tag:** *won't break my vows*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always pray before a hunt."

- **Frontier hook:** What vow did you make, and who did you make it to?

### 9. Doomed

> A seer, a vampire, or a doctor told you how you'll die. You believe them.

- **Pool:** +2 Pneuma

- **Power tag:** *nothing left to lose*

- **Weakness tag:** *death rides beside me*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always volunteer for the worst job."

- **Frontier hook:** How will you die, and what must you finish first?

### 10. Feral

> Raised in the wastes, by animals, mutants, or no one at all.

- **Pool:** +2 Akmē

- **Power tag:** *senses of a beast*

- **Weakness tag:** *forgets my manners*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always sleep with my back to a wall."

- **Frontier hook:** What raised you, and is it still out there?

### 11. Frontier-Hard

> Born in a fortified village and raised on dust, chores, and sunset curfews.

- **Pool:** +2 Sōma

- **Power tag:** *grew up on the Frontier*

- **Weakness tag:** *slow to trust strangers*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always bar the door at sunset."

- **Frontier hook:** What happened to your home village?

### 12. Gentle

> Kindness is rare on the Frontier. You haven't lost yours, yet.

- **Pool:** +2 Pneuma

- **Power tag:** *people open up to me*

- **Weakness tag:** *can't kill without cause*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always offer the enemy a chance to surrender."

- **Frontier hook:** Who did you spare, and did they deserve it?

### 13. Greedy

> Hunting is a job. Jobs pay. Better jobs pay more.

- **Pool:** +2 Psychē

- **Power tag:** *knows what everything's worth*

- **Weakness tag:** *a price for everything*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always search the bodies."

- **Frontier hook:** What are you saving up for?

### 14. Hollow-Eyed

> You've seen what vampires do in their estates. Nothing frightens you anymore, not even in your sleep.

- **Pool:** +2 Psychē

- **Power tag:** *seen too much to scare*

- **Weakness tag:** *nightmares every night*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always stay awake on the first watch."

- **Frontier hook:** What did you see in the estate?

### 15. Mutated

> The old wars and the old-world experiments left their mark on your bloodline: scales, extra senses, a too-long arm, eyes that glow.

- **Pool:** +2 Sōma

- **Power tag:** *body of the wastes*

- **Weakness tag:** *they call me monster*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always wear the hood in town."

- **Frontier hook:** What is your mutation, and who first called you monster?

### 16. Reckless

> Waiting gets people killed too. You'd rather it be on your terms.

- **Pool:** +2 Akmē

- **Power tag:** *first through the door*

- **Weakness tag:** *doesn't wait*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always charge when they hesitate."

- **Frontier hook:** Who died because you didn't wait?

### 17. Scarred

> Claw marks, burns, a missing finger. Every one is a story and a lesson.

- **Pool:** +2 Sōma

- **Power tag:** *survived worse than this*

- **Weakness tag:** *old wounds ache*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always check the wound before the pain."

- **Frontier hook:** Which scar do you never talk about?

### 18. Silent

> You speak rarely. People fill the silence with the truth.

- **Pool:** +2 Akmē

- **Power tag:** *says nothing, sees everything*

- **Weakness tag:** *people don't know where they stand*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always listen before I speak."

- **Frontier hook:** Why did you stop talking?

### 19. Superstitious

> Garlic in your pockets, salt across the doorway, a charm for every danger. Some of them even work.

- **Pool:** +2 Psychē

- **Power tag:** *knows every folk charm*

- **Weakness tag:** *jumps at shadows*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always follow the old rules: no whistling after dark, no mirrors covered."

- **Frontier hook:** Which charm saved your life, and who taught it to you?

### 20. Vengeful

> Someone took everything from you. You'll take it back, in blood.

- **Pool:** +2 Sōma

- **Power tag:** *I don't forget*

- **Weakness tag:** *will drop anything for revenge*

- **Suggested Instinct:** "Always ask strangers about the one I'm hunting."

- **Frontier hook:** Who are you hunting, and what did they take?
