> **Roll:** 2d12 + Skill + Tags (max +2) ± Status vs. Difficulty: **Easy 12 · Standard 15 · Hard 18 · Formidable 21 · Heroic 24**. Each Truth shifts Difficulty ±3.
> **Meet:** Success, 1 Power (+1 per full 3 over). **Miss by 1–3:** Success at a Cost. **Miss by 4+:** Failure, GM +1 Threat.
> **Doubles:** a success gets +1 Power; a failure or cost becomes a complication. Double 12s add +2 Power. Unspent Power goes to party Momentum.
