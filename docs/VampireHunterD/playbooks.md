## 1. Playbook Rules

### 1.1 What a Playbook Gives You

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Base pools** | Vigor / Finesse / Mind / Spirit, totalling 36. Add 4 points of your choice and +2 from your Descriptor |
| **Edge** | 3 points: the fixed Edge listed, plus 1 point from the listed choices |
| **Anchor skill** | Must be rated +3 or +4 in your pyramid |
| **Suggested skills** | A recommended pyramid; change it freely |
| **Playbook tags** | Choose **2** power tags from the playbook's list |
| **XP trigger** | One playbook behaviour that earns XP (proposal §12.1) |
| **Special abilities** | 1 free at creation; up to 2 more at creation for 1 Refresh each |
| **Starting equipment** | Signature items (choose 1 of 2), playbook kit, and always-carried items |
| **BIG examples** | Sample Beliefs, Goals, and Instincts |
| **Relationships** | 1 ally and 1 rival from the playbook's list |

### 1.2 Ability Notation

- **★ Major** — costs **1 Refresh** whenever you take it (creation or advancement).
- **◆ Capstone** — unlocked after **12 Advances** if you have **at least 4 abilities from this playbook**. Costs no Refresh. One capstone per character.
- **Veteran abilities:** after 6 Advances, you may take an ability from another playbook as an Advance. It costs 1 Refresh in addition to any ★ cost.

### 1.3 Rules References Used in Abilities

| Term | Meaning (proposal section) |
|---|---|
| **Power** | Successes generated by a roll; spent on Effects (§2.5, §5) |
| **Effort** | Spend 3 − Edge from a pool for a bonus die (before rolling) or +1 Power (after a success) (§2.4) |
| **Resist** | Roll 2d12, pay pool points by highest die − Edge, reduce a status by 2 tiers (§6) |
| **Status** | Tiered condition 1–6; tier 6 = Taken Out (§5.1) |
| **Truth** | Scene, site, region, or campaign tag that shifts Difficulty by ±3 (§8) |
| **Disposition** | Conflict "hit points" (§10) |
| **Checks / Grind** | Players' Turn currency and the hardship clock (Module B) |

## 2. Equipment Rules

### 2.1 Load (Blades in the Dark)

Before a mission, choose your **load**. During play, declare items from your playbook kit as you need them and mark their load.

| Load | Capacity | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Light** | 3 | You blend in; nothing obvious marks you as armed or burdened |
| **Normal** | 5 | You look ready for trouble |
| **Heavy** | 7 | You're *encumbered*: Finesse Effort costs +1 and Stealth Difficulty is one step higher |

- **Signature items** are chosen at creation and always count against load when carried (most are 1–2).
- **Always-carried items** (load 0) are always on you.
- Items marked **(0)** cost no load.

### 2.2 Gear as Tags

- Every item is a **power tag** when it's relevant, e.g. *crowbar* helps Athletics to force a door. Item tags count toward the **+2 tag cap**.
- **Weapons** also give one or more **conflict action tags** (proposal §10.6), e.g. *+1 Attack*.
- **Quality:** a fine or advanced item gives one extra benefit listed in its entry (it doesn't raise the cap).

### 2.3 Armour and Shields

| Item | Load | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Light armour** | 1 | *Armour 1*: when you Resist physical harm, reduce it by 1 extra tier |
| **Heavy armour** | 2 | *Armour 2*: reduce by 2 extra tiers; Finesse Effort +1 while worn |
| **Shield / riot shield / energy barrier** | 1 | *+1 Defend* in Fight conflicts; counts as cover outside conflicts |

Armour that absorbs a tier-4+ hit is *damaged* (−1 Armour) until repaired during downtime.

### 2.4 Common Gear (any playbook)

| Load | Fantasy | Modern | Sci-Fi |
|---|---|---|---|
| (0) | Belt knife, flint & steel, coin purse | Phone, wallet, keys, pocketknife | Comm unit, credchip, multitool |
| 1 | Rope (15 m), lantern & oil, trail rations (2 days) | Flashlight, first-aid kit, water & snacks | Glow rod, medpatch ×2, ration bars |
| 1 | Bedroll & blanket | Duffel with spare clothes | Thermal sheet & sleep sack |
| 1 | Crowbar or pry bar | Crowbar or bolt cutters | Hydraulic pry tool |
| 2 | Tent or tarp | Tent or camping kit | Pop-up shelter |

## 3. Bruiser

> Muscle, grit, and a willingness to stand in front of trouble. Soldiers, brawlers, bouncers, gladiators, mercenaries, security officers.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Base pools (V/F/M/S)** | 12 / 10 / 6 / 8 |
| **Edge** | Vigor 2, plus 1 in Finesse or Spirit |
| **Anchor skill** | Fight or Brawl |
| **Suggested skills** | +4 Fight · +3 Athletics, Endure · +2 Brawl, Intimidate, Shoot · +1 Notice, Will, Survival, Medicine |
| **XP trigger** | Address a challenge with force or threats |

**Playbook tags (choose 2):** *built like a wall* · *veteran of the line* · *scarred and unbothered* · *iron grip* · *knows how a fight will go* · *the look that stops trouble*

### 3.1 Special Abilities

| # | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Juggernaut** | When you Resist physical harm with Vigor, reduce the status by **3 tiers** instead of 2. |
| 2 | **Brutal** | Once per roll, when you spend Power on Attack with Fight or Brawl, the status gains **+1 tier for free**. |
| 3 | **Bodyguard** | When an ally within reach would suffer a consequence, you may take it instead. If you Resist it, your Edge counts as 1 higher for that roll. |
| 4 | **Cleave** | When you attack a group or minions, you get **one free Extra Feat** per roll (hit a second target, break their line, and so on). |
| 5 | **Menacing** | You may use **Vigor** for Intimidate Effort and Resistance. When you succeed on Intimidate against minions, spend 1 Momentum to make them flee, surrender, or freeze. |
| 6 | **Pain Tolerance** | Ignore the roll penalty from harmful physical statuses of tier 1–2. At tier 3+, the penalty is reduced by 1. |
| 7 | **★ Berserker** | Once per scene, enter a fury: you get **+1 bonus die** (free) on Fight, Brawl, and Athletics rolls for the rest of the scene. You can't script Defend or retreat. At scene end, lose 2 Vigor or take *Weary*. |
| 8 | **★ Unstoppable** | Once per scene, when a status would reach tier 6 on you or a pool would hit 0, keep acting until the end of the scene. Then you're Taken Out as normal. |
| ◆ | **Legend of the Field** | Your reputation is a **Campaign Truth** (*"Everyone knows [name]"*). In Fight conflicts, enemy Disposition starts **−2**, and the GM pays +1 Threat for reinforcements while you stand. |

## 8. Adept

|---|---|

| **Edge** | Spirit 2 (Arcana Adepts) or Mind 2 (Tech Adepts), plus 1 in any other pool |
| **Anchor skill** | Arcana (magic, faith, psionics) or Tech (hacking, gadgets) |
| **Suggested skills** | +4 Arcana · +3 Lore, Will · +2 Notice, Medicine, Persuade · +1 Investigate, Empathy, Deceive, Tech |
| **XP trigger** | Address a challenge with your powers or gadgets |

**Playbook tags (choose 2):** *touched by the Other* · *disciplined mind* · *sees what others can't* · *blessed* · *tinkerer's genius* · *fluent in the old pacts*

### 8.1 Core Feature — Domain (free)

Every Adept has a **Domain**: a broad power tag describing what they can do (*fire*, *healing*, *telekinesis*, *shadows*, *machines*, *networks*, *the dead*, *weather*).

- **Workings:** within your Domain, you can attempt any effect with your anchor skill. The GM sets Difficulty by scope:

| Scope | Difficulty | Example (Domain: fire) |
|---|---|---|
| Minor, personal | Easy 12 | Light a candle, warm your hands |
| Significant | Standard 15 | Hurl a firebolt, melt a lock |
| Dramatic | Hard 18 | Wall of flame across a street |
| Spectacular | Formidable 21 | Set a ship ablaze |
| World-shaking | Heroic 24 (usually a Ritual) | Call down a firestorm |

- **Cost:** each working costs **1 point** from Spirit (Arcana) or Mind (Tech), in addition to any Effort.
- **Backlash:** doubles on a failure or cost cause a backlash. The GM picks a fitting status for you (*burned-2*, *drained-2*, *glitched-2*) or a wild effect, in addition to the normal complication.
- Your Domain counts as a power tag on workings (inside the +2 cap).

### 8.2 Special Abilities

| # | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Focused Channel** | Workings inside your Domain cost **0 pool points** if you don't use Effort on that roll. |
| 2 | **Ward** | You can create protective statuses (*warded-X*) with Enhance. While warded, an ally reduces supernatural or tech-based harm by **+1 tier** when Resisting. |
| 3 | **Sense the Unseen** | Once per scene, ask a free Discover question about **hidden supernatural or technological forces** nearby. You always notice when such forces are used near you. |
| 4 | **Overchannel** | When you use Effort for Power on a working, gain **+2 Power** instead of +1, but take *strained-1*. |
| 5 | **Ritual** | Given time, materials, and a safe place, run a working as a **progress clock** instead of a single roll (8 segments for Formidable, 12 for Heroic). Ritual results can create Site or Region Truths. |
| 6 | **Bound Servant** | You have a minor spirit, construct, or drone with one power tag and one weakness. It can scout (one free Discover per scene in places it can reach), carry messages, or handle simple tasks. It can't fight. |
| 7 | **★ Second Domain** | Gain a second Domain. Workings in it use the same rules. |
| 8 | **★ Unleash** | Once per scene, a working hits **every target** in an area or group: each one receives the full Attack effects you buy. Afterwards, take *Weary* or lose 3 points from your casting pool. |
| ◆ | **Ascendant** | Once per session, a Heroic-scope working succeeds automatically with **5 Power**, no ritual required. If you use it again before your next downtime, gain a Spirit or Mind Scar. |

## 10. Design Notes

### 10.1 Balance Targets

| Playbook | Main pool | Conflict role | Exploration role | Social role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bruiser | Vigor | Attack / Defend | Endurance, forcing obstacles | Intimidation |
| Operator | Finesse | Feint / Maneuver | Infiltration, security | Deception |
| Sage | Mind | Maneuver (Discover, analysis) | Investigation, healing | Expertise |
| Face | Spirit | Argument and Negotiation lead | Contacts, disguise | Persuasion |
| Wayfarer | Vigor/Finesse | Ranged Attack, ambush | Travel, Grind, tracking | Local knowledge |
| Adept | Spirit/Mind | Flexible (Domain) | Supernatural or tech access | Varies by Domain |

### 10.2 Things to Watch in Playtest

- **Discount stacking:** abilities that reduce Power costs (Silver Tongue, Analyze, Field Medic, Deadly Precision, Jury-Rig) are strong when stacked with Effort for Power. Cap at one cost reduction per Effect.
- **Free bonus dice** (Berserker, Animal Companion, Second Wind, Nobody Sees Me Coming) still count toward the +2 bonus die maximum.
- **Adept workings** are the most open-ended. If they overshadow other playbooks, increase the base working cost to 2 pool points, or let Focused Channel apply only to minor and significant workings.
- **Vehicle signature items** (Wayfarer, modern and sci-fi) are 0 load because they don't come on foot missions. Treat them as a story tag when they're not present.
- **★ count:** each playbook has two Major abilities and every capstone is free. Characters who take both ★ abilities will be at Refresh 1–2, as intended.
