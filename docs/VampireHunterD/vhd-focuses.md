# 2d12 System — Vampire Hunter D Setting Pack: Focuses (v0.1)

> Companion to **vhd-setting-rules.md**, **vhd-descriptors.md**, and **vhd-playbooks.md**.
> 20 setting Focuses that complete the character sentence:
> "I am a [Descriptor] [Playbook] **who [Focus]**."

---

## 1. Focus Rules

Focus rules are unchanged from **focuses.md** §1:

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Power tags** | 2 |
| **Weakness tag** | 1 |
| **PC link** | A prompt that connects you to another PC |
| **Tier 1 ability** | Fixed; gained at creation for free |
| **Tier 2 ability** | Choose 1 of 2; after 3 Advances, costs an Advance |
| **Tier 3 ability** | Choose 1 of 2; after 6 Advances, costs an Advance. Exactly one option is ★ Major (1 Refresh) |

- **◈ Weird** Focuses assume Noble blood, Noble science, or the supernatural. In this setting all are allowed; the marker shows which ones draw attention from Nobles, Occultists, and frightened villagers.
- **Generic Focuses:** the 24 Focuses in **focuses.md** also work here. Good fits are *Never Leaves Anyone Behind*, *Masters a Weapon*, *Moves Like a Shadow*, *Never Misses*, *Opens Every Lock*, *Talks Their Way Out of Anything*, *Lives Off the Land*, and *Made a Bargain With Something Old*. Use the setting Focuses below in place of *Hunts the Things in the Dark* and *Is More Than Human*.
- **Shared reminders:** free bonus dice count toward the +2 maximum; only one cost reduction applies to a single Effect.

## 3. Bloodline Focuses

### 1. Carries the Blood of the Nobility ◈

> Dhampir. Half-human, half-Noble. Stronger and faster than any human, hated by both sides, and never quite free of the sun or the thirst.

- **Power tags:** *the strength of the Nobility*, *eyes that see in the dark*
- **Weakness tag:** *half-blood* (when recognised, the scene gains *Feared and Despised*)
- **PC link:** "[PC] saw what I am and didn't run. I still don't know why."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Noble Blood** | You ignore darkness Truths and sense any Noble within sight. A short rest restores **2 + Edge** Vigor. You can't be *Kissed* or Turned. You're subject to **Sunlight Syndrome** and **the Thirst** (setting rules §7). |
| 2a | **Dhampir Regeneration** | Once per scene, lower one physical status by **2 tiers** (no roll). Thirst +1. |
| 2b | **The Noble Gaze** | You can use the Gaze: on a successful Will or Intimidate roll against a human or Lesser Noble who meets your eyes, inflict *entranced* as an Attack. Thirst +1 each time. In a Duel of Wills against a Noble, gain a free bonus die. |
| 3a | **Night Walker** | Under the **Night** Truth, your physical Attacks inflict **+1 tier**, and your blade counts as the beheading bane against a Noble with a tier-4+ physical status. |
| 3b | **★ Awaken the Blood** | Once per scene, let the Noble half rise (*Thirst*): until the scene ends, you get **+2 bonus dice** free on physical rolls, your Attacks inflict **+1 tier**, and your physical statuses can't go above tier 5. Afterwards, Thirst +2, and anyone who saw it treats you as a Noble. |

**Table options for Dhampirs (choose at session zero):**

- **Rare blood:** only one PC in the party can take this Focus.
- **Costly blood:** this Focus costs **1 Refresh** at creation.
- **The sun is merciless:** Sun-Sick gains +2 per scene instead of +1 (setting rules §7.1).

### 2. Was Kissed and Lived ◈

> A Noble fed on you for nights, then was destroyed, or left, or let you go. You're still human. Mostly.

- **Power tags:** *the Call has no hold on me*, *I know how they think*
- **Weakness tag:** *the Noble is still out there* (or *its blood is still in me*)
- **PC link:** "[PC] sat with me through the nights when I nearly turned."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Survivor of the Kiss** | *Kissed* gains **1 fewer tier** on you. You always know when a Noble uses the Call or the Gaze within earshot. |
| 2a | **Blood Bond** | At night you sense the direction of the Noble that bit you (or its bloodline). It can sense you too; the GM may spend 1 Threat to use this against you. |
| 2b | **One of Them, Almost** | Lesser Nobles and Noble servants mistake you for one of their own. Deceive rolls to pass among them are **two steps easier**. |
| 3a | **Unbroken** | When you Resist the Gaze, the Call, or the Kiss with Spirit, reduce the status by **3 tiers**, and the cost is 0 on a highest die of 5+. |
| 3b | **★ Turn the Bond** | Once per session, in a Duel of Wills against a Noble of the bloodline that bit you, gain a free bonus die on every roll. If you win, you also learn where it sleeps. |

### 3. Carries a Passenger ◈

> Something lives in you: a face in your palm, an eye on your shoulder, a voice only you can hear. It's old, rude, and hungry, and it has saved your life more than once.

- **Power tags:** *a voice that knows things*, *it eats what hurts me*
- **Weakness tag:** *it has its own plans*
- **PC link:** "[PC] is the only one the Passenger likes. That worries me."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Passenger** | Once per scene, ask a free Discover: the Passenger answers, truthfully but not always helpfully. It can talk to others, which is often a compel. |
| 2a | **Devourer** | Once per scene, the Passenger swallows a hostile effect it can reach (fire, gas, mist, a working, a cypher): **Disrupt** it for 0 Power. The GM may compel its appetite afterwards. |
| 2b | **Feed Me, Heal You** | In the Players' Turn, spend 1 Check to feed it (earth, water, fire, or air). Next session, once per scene, it lowers one of your physical statuses by 2 tiers. |
| 3a | **Older Than the Nobility** | Once per scene, take a free bonus die on a Noble Lore or Occult roll. The Passenger remembers the age when the Nobility were young. |
| 3b | **★ Let It Drive** | Once per session, let the Passenger take your body for one action: it succeeds automatically with **4 Power**. Then the GM names the price it demands (a meal, a favour, a secret, a scene where it's in charge). |

## 4. Hunter Focuses

### 4. Hunts the Nobility

> Your trade is the hardest one on the Frontier: you kill Nobles.

- **Power tags:** *knows where they sleep*, *a stake for every heart*
- **Weakness tag:** *the Nobility know my name*
- **PC link:** "[PC] was there for my first Noble kill. It went badly."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Noble Hunter** | Once per scene, Discover one of a Noble's weakness tags without rolling. |
| 2a | **Bane Mastery** | Your bane strikes (setting rules §5.2) are **Standard 15** instead of Hard 18. |
| 2b | **Steel Against the Gaze** | When you Resist the Gaze or the Call with Spirit, the cost is **0 on a highest die of 5+**. |
| 3a | **Turn Weakness to Bane** | Upgrading a Noble's weakness tag into a bane (setting rules §8.1) costs **2 Power** instead of 3. |
| 3b | **★ The Hunter's Mark** | Once per scene, mark a Noble you can see. Until the scene ends, all allies' Attacks against it inflict **+1 tier**, and it can't use *Mist Form* to escape. |

### 5. Collects the Bounty

> A Hunter is a professional. You take the job, you finish it, and you get paid.

- **Power tags:** *always gets paid*, *proof in a sack*
- **Weakness tag:** *money first*
- **PC link:** "[PC] owes me their share of the last bounty."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Professional** | At the start of each contract, the party gains **+1 Momentum**. Once per contract, ask a free Discover: "What isn't the client telling us?" |
| 2a | **Beat the Competition** | When rival Hunters race you for a bounty (setting rules §10.4), their project clock ticks **1 less** each World Turn, and you gain +1 Advance on any roll that gets ahead of them. |
| 2b | **Bring Them In Alive** | Your Attacks can inflict *subdued* instead of harm. A target Taken Out by *subdued* is captured, and a living quarry is worth **one step more** bounty. |
| 3a | **My Reputation Precedes Me** | Your reputation tag (setting rules §10.3) counts as a free extra tag in Negotiation, ignoring the cap. Clients always pay half up front. |
| 3b | **★ The Big Score** | Once per session, when you complete a contract, choose one extra reward: an artifact from the quarry's hoard, a Region Truth in your favour, or a second *Noble's bounty*. |

### 6. Hunts the Beasts of the Frontier

> Not every monster is a Noble. Sand sharks, werewolves, mutant spiders, Noble bioweapons gone feral: you hunt them all.

- **Power tags:** *knows every monster*, *nets, poison, and patience*
- **Weakness tag:** *chases the trophy*
- **PC link:** "[PC] pulled me out of a beast's jaws. I still have the tooth."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Beast Lore** | Once per scene, Discover a monster's weakness tag (not a Noble's) without rolling. |
| 2a | **Baited Trap** | Spend 1 Power to Create *Baited Trap*. A beast that enters it is *Impeded* until it breaks free (an Athletics roll vs. Hard 18). |
| 2b | **Harvest** | After killing a monster, spend a scene to harvest it for one cypher (tier 2–3) that matches its power: venom, hide, a gland, an eye. |
| 3a | **Bane of Beasts** | Your Attacks against monsters inflict **+1 tier**. |
| 3b | **★ Monster-Slayer** | In Fights against monsters (not Nobles), the enemy's Disposition starts **−3**, and minion beasts flee after your first successful Attack. |

### 7. Seeks Revenge on a Noble

> A Noble took your family, your village, or your life as it was. You'll find it, and you'll end it.

- **Power tags:** *my hate keeps me warm*, *I've studied it for years*
- **Weakness tag:** *I'll sacrifice anything for this*
- **PC link:** "[PC] knows who I'm hunting, and promised to help me."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The One** | Name your Noble. You always know when you're within a day's ride of it, and once per session ask a free Discover about it. |
| 2a | **Cold Fury** | When you Resist the named Noble's Gaze, Call, or fear, the cost is **0**. |
| 2b | **Nothing Held Back** | When you burn a tag against the named Noble or its servants, gain **+2 Power** instead of +1. |
| 3a | **Its Servants Die First** | The named Noble's servants and Barbarois start conflicts against you with **−2 Disposition**. |
| 3b | **★ This Ends Tonight** | When you face the named Noble, gain a free bonus die on every roll against it, and your bane strikes are **Standard 15**. If it escapes you, gain a Spirit Scar. |

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Astral Self** | Leave your body as an unseen astral form (range about 1 km). You use Occult for Stealth and Notice. Your body is helpless and *Impeded* while you're out. Returning costs 1 Spirit. |
| 2a | **Astral Strike** | Your astral form can Attack with Occult, inflicting mental statuses (*terrified*, *dazed*) even on Nobles. |

| Tier | Ability | Effect |

| 2a | **Consecrate** | In a scene of prayer, bless water or stakes. Bane strikes with them inflict **+1 tier**, and holy water is an Attack (*burning*) against Nobles. |
| 2b | **Last Rites** | Once per scene, every ally who can hear your prayer restores 2 Spirit, and Resists the Call with Edge +1 until the scene ends. |
| 3a | **Faith Unbroken** | When you Resist fear, the Gaze, or possession with Spirit, the cost is **0 on a highest die of 5+**. |
| 3b | **★ Holy Ground** | Once per session, sanctify a place: create the Truth *Holy Ground* for the scene. Noble powers don't work there, and every Noble Threat spend costs **+1**. |

## 10. Design Notes

- **Dhampir as a Focus** lets any playbook be half-Noble: a Dhampir Sellsword is the classic Hunter, but a Dhampir Frontier Doctor or Dhampir Broker plays very differently. The Tier 1 package is strong (darkvision, Noble-sense, regeneration, immunity to the Kiss) because Sunlight Syndrome and the Thirst are real costs. Use the table options if it outshines other Focuses.
- **Overlaps with playbooks:** *Heals the Kissed* stacks with the Frontier Doctor's *Treat the Kiss*, and *Holds the Line Until Dawn* stacks with the Warden. Both stacks are intended specialist builds; none of them reduce the same Power cost twice.
- **Noble-specific Focuses** (*Hunts the Nobility*, *Seeks Revenge on a Noble*) rely on bane strikes. If a campaign has few Nobles, steer players toward *Hunts the Beasts of the Frontier* or *Collects the Bounty*.
- **★ options** are all at Tier 3, as in **focuses.md**, so a character can always choose a path that keeps their Refresh.
