## 1. Sellsword

> Muscle for hire. You stand between the village and the thing coming out of the dark, and you hold the stake when it's time.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Base pools (V/F/M/S)** | 12 / 10 / 6 / 8 |
| **Edge** | Vigor 2, plus 1 in Finesse or Spirit |
| **Anchor skill** | Fight or Brawl |
| **Suggested skills** | +4 Fight · +3 Athletics, Endure · +2 Brawl, Intimidate, Shoot · +1 Notice, Will, Frontier, Ride |
| **XP trigger** | Address a challenge with force or threats |

**Playbook tags (choose 2):** *built like a wall* · *veteran of a dozen hunts* · *scarred and unbothered* · *iron grip* · *knows how a fight will go* · *the look that empties a saloon*

### 1.1 Special Abilities

| # | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Juggernaut** | When you Resist physical harm with Vigor, reduce the status by **3 tiers** instead of 2. |
| 2 | **Stake Driver** | When you make a **bane strike** (setting rules §5.2) with Fight or Brawl, gain a **free bonus die**. On a success, the Noble can't spend Threat on *Rise From the Coffin* this session. |
| 3 | **Bodyguard** | When an ally within reach would suffer a consequence, you may take it instead. If you Resist it, your Edge counts as 1 higher for that roll. This includes a Noble's bite: you can take the Kiss in their place. |
| 4 | **Wade Through Them** | When you attack a group or minions (wolves, Barbarois packs, turned villagers), you get **one free Extra Feat** per roll. |
| 5 | **Menacing** | You may use **Vigor** for Intimidate Effort and Resistance. When you succeed on Intimidate against minions or a mob, spend 1 Momentum to make them flee, surrender, or freeze. |
| 6 | **Pain Tolerance** | Ignore the roll penalty from harmful physical statuses of tier 1–2. At tier 3+, the penalty is reduced by 1. This also applies to *Sun-Sick*. |
| 7 | **★ Blood Fury** | Once per scene, enter a fury: you get **+1 bonus die** (free) on Fight, Brawl, and Athletics rolls for the rest of the scene. You can't script Defend or retreat. At scene end, lose 2 Vigor or take *Weary*. If you have Noble blood, gain +1 Thirst. |
| 8 | **★ Last One Standing** | Once per scene, when a status would reach tier 6 on you or a pool would hit 0, keep acting until the end of the scene. Then you're Taken Out as normal. |
| ◆ | **A Name the Nobles Know** | Your reputation is a **Campaign Truth** (*"Nobles have heard of [name]"*). In Fight conflicts, Noble and servant Disposition starts **−2**. Once per session, a Noble's pride compel against you costs nothing. |

## 7. Mutant

### 7.1 Core Feature — Mutations (free)

Choose **2 Mutations**. Each is a power tag that ignores load, plus a rule. Every Mutation is visible unless you hide it (a hood, a cloak, a Deceive roll). When it's seen, the scene gains *Feared and Despised*.

## 10. Design Notes

### 10.1 Balance Targets

| Playbook | Main pool | Conflict role | Exploration role | Social role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sellsword | Vigor | Attack / Defend, bane strikes | Forcing obstacles | Intimidation |
| Stalker | Finesse | Feint / Maneuver | Castles by day, security | Deception |
| Frontier Doctor | Mind | Maneuver (Discover), Restore | Investigation, healing, the Kiss | Expertise, trust |
| Broker | Spirit | Negotiation and Argument lead | Rumours, contacts | Persuasion, contracts |
| Rider | Vigor/Finesse | Ranged Attack, Chase, Hunt | Travel, Grind, tracking | Local knowledge |
| Occultist | Spirit/Mind | Flexible (Domain), Duel of Wills | Supernatural and Noble-science access | Varies by Domain |
| Mutant | Vigor/Finesse | Attack / Maneuver | Climbing, hiding, wastelands | Monsters and servants |
| Cyborg Hunter | Vigor | Ranged Attack, heavy firepower | Noble machines, castles | Intimidation |
| Warden | Vigor/Mind | Siege of the Night, Defend | Preparation, fortification | Villagers, local law |

### 10.2 Things to Watch in Playtest

- **Mutant and Cyborg core features** each grant two tagged abilities for free, more than other playbooks. They're balanced by *Feared and Despised* (Mutant) and glitches and a low Spirit pool (Cyborg). If they outshine others, drop to 1 free Mutation or Implant.
- **Undying vs. More Machine Than Man:** the Cyborg capstone is strong by design at 12 Advances. Watch that it doesn't end the threat of mundane harm entirely.
- **Warden** is strongest in sieges and weaker on castle raids. Make sure the campaign has both, or let the Warden's traps travel (Trapmaker works anywhere).
- **Free bonus dice** (Stake Driver, Horse-Bond, Targeting eye, Combat Mode, Full Transformation) still count toward the +2 bonus die maximum.
- **★ count:** each playbook has two Major abilities and a free capstone, as in **playbooks.md**.
