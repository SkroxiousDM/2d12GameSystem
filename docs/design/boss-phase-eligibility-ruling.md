# Explicit phased-boss eligibility

Date: 2026-11-07. Branch: `draft/conflict-system-rewrite`.

Status: approved eligibility decision recorded in `boss-phases-fixed-thresholds-draft.md`. Not integrated into active Adversaries source by this commit. Neither this decision nor the approved reinforcement accounting activates or approves the remaining Boss Phases proposal.

## Designation

The GM may mark a Great or Paramount adversary as a Phased Boss. Rank and the Leader role do not grant phases automatically. Before calculating starting Disposition or seeing its roll, designate at most one eligible phased boss per side and record it on the card or conflict record.

The designation is fixed for that conflict. It cannot be added, transferred or replaced after setup, including through reinforcement arrival or the boss leaving. A side may have no phased boss.

Minion groups, Minor and Established adversaries require an explicit exception. A full-PC rival requires an explicit phase-capable entry rather than fictional importance alone.

If setup ends in overwhelming preparation or simultaneous setup defeat, do not activate phases.

## Multiple bosses and participation

Other powerful adversaries retain their own ratings, Role traits and Specials, but gain no phase benefit unless it expressly affects them. Phase thresholds refer to shared side Disposition, not personal tracks. Do not split a coordinated side merely to obtain more phase systems; separate sides need genuinely distinct objectives and independent participation.

When the designated boss cannot participate, its designation remains recorded and is not inherited. Designation grants no permission to act while Taken Out, return, or use benefits while unable to contribute. A lawful return to the same unresolved conflict retains the designation and phase history without resetting setup or used phase triggers. Exact benefit activation/resumption remains a separate participation decision.

## No automatic extras

Designation alone adds no Grit, starting Disposition, Threat, Woe, turn, script change, reinforcement or unlisted Special. It grants no immunity to defeat, removal or finishing procedures, and no claim that encounter cost is balanced. Benefits require a separately approved phase procedure; their numerical impact remains unplaytested.

## Example

A Paramount guardian and Great human commander share a side. The GM designates the guardian before setup. The commander keeps its Leader trait and ordinary Specials but receives no personal phase progression. If the guardian is removed, the commander does not inherit designation.
