- Last Stand (§6.10), implemented in `b833e4097d81ec9b45a1a15e2cfa288478965816`: 2 Woe postpones eligible defeat at 0 shared Disposition for exactly one final volley for both sides under normal interactions, scripting and continuous rotation. No chained extensions, new phase activation, free script change or exchange refresh; finishing and settlement wait until the extension ends.
- Fail-safe (§6.11), implemented in the same commit: 3 Woe, an eligible active Guardian and final defeat at 0 shared Disposition are required after any Last Stand. Setup defeat, concession, mutual defeat and inability-only defeat do not trigger it.
- Feed and Shock Field (§6.12), implemented in the same commit: each qualifying paid activation records an outcome-independent deferred Bled-2 or jolted-2 application. Pending records cause no status penalty, stacking or removal. Resolve applications individually in trigger order after the final outcome and permitted finishing, with eligible Resistance/reduction before stacking; then credit eligible remaining injuries once and settle compromise. Concessions acknowledge rather than waive the imposed effects. Feed retains its outside-conflict effect and recovery when used; Shock Field retains the broader melee versus-loss trigger. Fail-safe remains a separate compromise procedure.
