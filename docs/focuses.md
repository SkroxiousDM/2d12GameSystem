# 2d12 System — Focus List (v0.2)

> Companion to **2d12-system-proposal.md** and **playbooks.md**. 24 setting-neutral Focuses that complete the character sentence:
> "I am a [Descriptor] [Playbook] **who [Focus]**."

---

## 1. Focus Rules

### 1.1 What a Focus Gives You

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Power tags** | 2 |
| **Weakness tag** | 1 (can be compelled for Fate Points) |
| **PC link** | A prompt that connects you to another player character |
| **Tier 1 ability** | Fixed; gained at creation for free |
| **Tier 2 ability** | **Choose 1 of 2**; after 3 Advances, costs an Advance |
| **Tier 3 ability** | **Choose 1 of 2**; after 6 Advances, costs an Advance |

- **The other option:** once you've reached a tier, you may take its unchosen option later as a normal Advance.
- **★ Major:** costs 1 Refresh when taken (proposal §4.1). Every Focus has exactly one ★ option, always at Tier 3, so you can always choose a path that keeps your Refresh.
- **Any playbook can take any Focus.** The "Suits" line is a suggestion.
- **Changing Focus:** at a major milestone, with GM approval, you can swap your Focus. You keep Tier 1 of the new one and rebuy higher tiers with Advances.

### 1.2 Weird Focuses ◈

Focuses marked **◈ Weird** assume the supernatural, impossible technology, or both. In mundane settings the GM removes them from the list. Every other Focus works in any genre.

### 1.3 Shared Rules Reminders

- Free bonus dice from Focuses still count toward the **+2 bonus die maximum**.
- Abilities that add bonus above the tag cap only do so for the tag they name, and are limited to **+1 above the normal +2 cap (maximum +3)** unless stated otherwise.
- Only **one cost reduction** applies to a single Effect (e.g. you can't combine two "costs 1 Power instead of 2" abilities).

## 3. Combat Focuses

### 1. Never Leaves Anyone Behind

> Loyal to a fault. You go back for the fallen, every time.

- **Power tags:** *I've got your back*, *carry them out*
- **Weakness tag:** *can't walk away*
- **PC link:** "I once failed to save someone. [PC] reminds me of them."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shield Bearer** | When an ally next to you takes a status, spend 1 point from any pool to reduce it by **1 tier** before they Resist. |
| 2a | **Second Wind** | Once per scene, when an ally is Taken Out, you may act immediately to reach them. Your next roll to help or rescue them gets a **free bonus die**. |
| 2b | **Interpose** | When you accept an injury to cancel Disposition loss from an Attack or Feint, cancel **1 additional** Disposition loss, up to the loss inflicted. All normal injury-trade restrictions apply. |
| 3a | **No One Left** | Once per session, when the party retreats or escapes, no one is left behind or captured. Each ally clears one tier-1 or tier-2 status. |
| 3b | **★ Living Wall** | Once per scene, until your next action, all harm aimed at allies within reach hits you instead. You Resist each with Edge +1. |

### 2. Leads From the Front

> Captain, chief, or sergeant: people follow you.

- **Power tags:** *born to command*, *they believe in me*
- **Weakness tag:** *every loss is on me*
- **PC link:** "[PC] was the first person who ever followed my lead."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rally** | Once per scene, spend 1 Momentum to restore **2 points** to one pool of every ally who can hear you. |
| 2a | **Battle Plan** | At the start of a conflict, before scripting, look at **one** of the opposing side's scripted actions for the first exchange. |
| 2b | **Lead by Example** | When you succeed with **3+ Power**, the next ally who acts toward the same goal gets a **free bonus die**. |
| 3a | **★ Warband** | Gain a loyal crew (a group with 2 power tags and 1 weakness). It acts as one group, gives free Help once per scene, or holds a position. It takes statuses as a single target. |
| 3b | **Unbreakable Line** | Once per conflict, when your side's Disposition would drop to 0, it stays at **1** and you take a tier-2 status. |

### 3. Masters a Weapon

> Years of drill with one weapon: a blade, a rifle, a staff, bare fists.

- **Power tags:** *one with my [weapon]*, *drilled every day*
- **Weakness tag:** *lost without it*
- **PC link:** "[PC] once saw me lose a duel, and kept it secret."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Signature Style** | Choose a weapon type. That weapon's tag may add **+1 above the normal +2 cap (maximum +3 from tags)**, and it gains a second conflict action tag (e.g. a sword also gives +1 Defend). |
| 2a | **Riposte** | In Fight conflicts, when you win a versus roll with Defend, you may spend any of that Power to remove the opponent's Disposition instead of restoring your own. |
| 2b | **Disarming Master** | The Disarm maneuver costs **2 Power** instead of 3. Outside conflicts, Weaken can strip an opponent's weapon tag for the scene. |
| 3a | **Perfect Strike** | Once per scene, after rolling with your weapon, turn one kept die into a **12**. If that creates doubles, apply them. |
| 3b | **★ Weapon Saint** | Your weapon becomes an **artifact** (Module A) with one extra power tag and an ability you design with the GM. It never depletes and costs no attunement Refresh. |

### 4. Fights Dirty

> Honour is for people who can afford to lose.

- **Power tags:** *no rules in a fight*, *eye for a cheap shot*
- **Weakness tag:** *no one trusts me in a fair fight*
- **PC link:** "I taught [PC] that winning is the only thing that matters. They're still deciding if I was right."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cheap Shot** | Once per scene, as part of an Attack, Create a story tag (*sand in the eyes*, *low blow*) for **1 Power**. |
| 2a | **Improvised Arsenal** | Anything in reach is a weapon for you: chairs, bottles, tools. It gives **+1 Attack or +1 Maneuver** and costs no load. |
| 2b | **Kick Them While They're Down** | Against a target with a harmful status of tier 2+, gain **+1 Power** on Attack. |
| 3a | **Every Trick in the Book** | In Fight conflicts, your Feints remove **+1 Disposition**. |
| 3b | **★ Nothing Is Off-Limits** | Once per session, declare a hazard Truth that was already there (*gas leak*, *rotten floor*, *loose cargo*). Your next roll that uses it gets a **free Extra Feat**. |

## 4. Stealth & Precision Focuses

### 5. Moves Like a Shadow

> Silent, patient, and impossible to pin down.

- **Power tags:** *silent as snowfall*, *at home in darkness*
- **Weakness tag:** *uneasy in the light*
- **PC link:** "[PC] is the only one who always knows where I am."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shadow-Born** | Truths about darkness, noise, or crowds lower your Stealth Difficulty by **two steps** instead of one. |
| 2a | **Vanish** | Once per scene, after any roll, become hidden if there's any cover nearby. No roll needed. |
| 2b | **Shadow Escort** | Up to **two allies** can use your Stealth result instead of rolling their own. |
| 3a | **Unseen Hand** | Any action you take while hidden gains **+1 Power** on a success. |
| 3b | **★ Living Shadow** | Once per session, pass through a guarded or blocked area unnoticed (no roll) and arrive at a point you choose. |

### 6. Never Misses

> Sniper, archer, gunslinger: steady breath and a sharp eye.

- **Power tags:** *steady breath*, *eye like a hawk*
- **Weakness tag:** *clumsy up close*
- **PC link:** "[PC] owes their life to a shot I made."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Called Shot** | Once per roll with Shoot, a **free Weaken** effect: burn one of the target's tags (weapon, cover, mobility) for the scene. |
| 2a | **Overwatch** | Once per scene, cancel a status of tier 2 or less about to hit an ally by shooting the source. In conflicts, you may Defend with **Shoot** when covering fire can protect your side. |
| 2b | **Trick Shot** | Ignore Truths about cover and angles. A free **Extra Feat** when you shoot objects (cut a rope, hit a switch, ricochet). |
| 3a | **One Shot** | Once per session, a Shoot Attack against a non-major NPC **Takes them Out** on a success. |
| 3b | **★ Sniper's Patience** | Spend a scene setting up (4-segment clock). Your shot gets **+2 bonus dice** for free and **+2 Power** on a success. |

### 7. Opens Every Lock

> Locks, safes, firewalls, sealed doors: they're puzzles, and you love puzzles.

- **Power tags:** *nothing stays closed*, *thinks like an architect*
- **Weakness tag:** *can't resist a challenge*
- **PC link:** "[PC] keeps me out of trouble, or tries to."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bypass** | Each success on a lock, safe, or security clock Advances it **+1 extra segment**. |
| 2a | **Back Door** | Once per location, Create the Truth *I know a way in* for **1 Power**. |
| 2b | **Trap Sense** | You always notice traps and alarms before triggering them. Resisting traps costs **0 on a highest die of 5+**. |
| 3a | **Master Key** | Ordinary locks open without a roll. Complex security is **two Difficulty steps lower**. |
| 3b | **★ Vault Breaker** | Once per mission, declare a security layer already cracked: fill one security clock completely. |

### 8. Always Has an Escape Plan

> You never walk into a room without knowing how to leave it.

- **Power tags:** *knows every exit*, *slippery*
- **Weakness tag:** *runs first, thinks later*
- **PC link:** "[PC] was with me during my worst getaway."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Exit Strategy** | When you enter a new scene, ask one free Discover question about exits or escape routes. |
| 2a | **Clean Getaway** | In Chase conflicts where you're fleeing, your side gets **+3 Disposition**. |
| 2b | **Contingency** | Once per mission, when things go wrong, reveal a prepared fallback (stashed gear, a getaway vehicle, a safe house) as a story tag. No Momentum needed. |
| 3a | **Not Here** | Once per session, escape any scene with every ally who can follow, before the next status lands. |
| 3b | **★ Dead Man's Switch** | You can't be held. When captured or Taken Out, you escape at the start of the next scene. The GM gains 2 Threat. |

## 5. Knowledge Focuses

### 9. Builds Impossible Things

> An artificer, engineer, or inventor who makes what shouldn't work, work.

- **Power tags:** *workshop hands*, *it'll work, trust me*
- **Weakness tag:** *never quite finished*
- **PC link:** "I built [PC]'s gear, and I'm still tinkering with it."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Jury-Rig** | Once per scene, Craft a temporary gadget (a story tag) that lasts the scene and costs **1 Power** to Create. |
| 2a | **Inventor** | During downtime, turn a gadget into a permanent item with a 6-segment clock. It gets 1 power tag and 1 action tag. |
| 2b | **Field Repairs** | Restore effects work on gear, vehicles, and constructs. Repair damaged armour without downtime. |
| 3a | **Masterwork** | Create one **artifact** (Module A) with a 12-segment downtime clock. You pay no Refresh to attune your own masterwork. |
| 3b | **★ Rig the Field** | Once per scene, rig the area with gadgets: Create **two** story tags for 2 Power total. |

### 10. Heals the Broken

> Doctor, field medic, herbalist, or combat surgeon.

- **Power tags:** *calm hands*, *bedside manner*
- **Weakness tag:** *can't refuse anyone in pain*
- **PC link:** "I patched up [PC] when no one else would."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Triage** | Once per scene, reduce an ally's harmful status by **1 tier** without a roll. |
| 2a | **Battlefield Surgeon** | In conflicts, you may Defend with **Medicine** when you can reach an ally and provide appropriate treatment. On a successful Defend, restore your side's Disposition normally and clear one tier-1 harmful status from that ally that your treatment can address. |
| 2b | **Remedy** | In each Players' Turn, clear one Grind condition (Module B) from one ally **without spending a Check**. |
| 3a | **Miracle Worker** | Once per session, bring an ally back from a tier-6 physical status. They return at tier 4 and are no longer Taken Out. |
| 3b | **★ Physician's Care** | Allies you treat during a short rest recover **all** pool points, not just 1 + Edge. |

### 11. Solves the Unsolvable

> Detective, investigator, or analyst: you follow the thread to the end.

- **Power tags:** *nothing gets past me*, *follows the thread*
- **Weakness tag:** *obsessed with the case*
- **PC link:** "[PC] was part of the case that made me."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Case File** | Start a **Mystery clock** (6 or 8) for a case. Every Discover you buy also Advances it 1 segment. When it fills, the GM reveals the core truth. |
| 2a | **Interrogator** | When questioning someone, each Discover gives **two answers**. |
| 2b | **Reconstruction** | At the scene of a past event, ask **three** Discover questions for free (once per location). |
| 3a | **The Reveal** | Once per session, name the culprit in front of others. If you're right, your side starts any conflict against them with **+4 Disposition**, and they get no compromise if they lose. |
| 3b | **★ One Step Ahead** | Once per session, reveal that you anticipated an opponent's move: **cancel one GM Threat spend** as it happens. |

### 12. Remembers What Was Forgotten

> Historian, archaeologist, or keeper of lost lore.

- **Power tags:** *walks among ruins*, *reads dead languages*
- **Weakness tag:** *lives in the past*
- **PC link:** "[PC] helped me recover something that was lost."

| Tier | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Echoes of the Past** | At any old or abandoned place, ask one free Discover question about its history. |
| 2a | **Lost Knowledge** | Once per session, you've read about this before: Create a story tag (*ancient weakness*, *forgotten route*) for **0 Power**. |
| 2b | **Relic Hunter** | When you find cyphers or treasure, roll or draw **twice** and choose. You identify artifacts automatically. |
| 3a | **Living Archive** | Lore rolls about history, legends, and old cultures are **two Difficulty steps lower**. |
| 3b | **★ Rewrite History** | Prove a Campaign Truth is false with a 12-segment research clock. When it fills, the Truth changes (Module C). |

Off-type combinations are encouraged: a *Face who Masters a Weapon* is a duellist-diplomat; an *Adept who Was Born Behind the Wheel* is a witch with a haunted truck.

### 9.2 Things to Watch in Playtest

- **Tag-cap exceptions** (Masters a Weapon, Is More Than Human): **resolved (v0.3)** — both abilities now add **+1 above the normal +2 cap (maximum +3 from tags)** instead of ignoring the cap outright. Re-test rolls at Formidable/Heroic Difficulty after this change to confirm it still feels earned rather than trivial.
- **Once-per-session "auto-wins"** (One Shot, The Reveal, Not Here, Become Them, The Right Person): these are deliberate spotlight moments. If several players have them, the GM may limit the table to one per scene.
- **Overlap with playbook abilities:** a few Focus abilities echo playbook abilities (Beast Lore / Monster Lore, Trap Sense / Escape Artist). That's intended for off-type characters; for on-type characters, the two don't stack. Take the better one.
- **★ distribution:** every Focus has exactly one ★ option, and it's never the Tier 1 ability, so you can always reach Tier 3 at full Refresh.
- **Gaps for v0.3:** a dedicated faith/priest Focus (non-Weird reading: "Keeps the Faith"), a scholar-of-war Focus, a merchant/trader Focus, and a performer/artist Focus.
