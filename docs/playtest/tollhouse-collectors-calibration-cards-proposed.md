# Tollhouse Collectors — proposed calibration cards

Status: **proposed for review**, not approved encounter data or recovered historical details.

These six cards preserve the original Minor action ratings, roles, and selected Specials from `docs/playtest/tollhouse-collectors-cards.md`. Equipment, Armour, tags, Harm, Other ratings, Tells, and objectives below are new proposals. Established variants retain the same equipment and Specials; their numerical changes follow the Established rank package.

The original-card reference and historical findings should remain unchanged.

## Shared encounter definition

The Collectors hold a neglected tollhouse where an old road crosses a narrow drainage cutting. They operate as an organised three-person crew, not a minion group.

| Field | Proposed definition |
|---|---|
| Opposition objective | Hold the crossing and seize one supply pack as the unpaid toll. |
| Hunter objective | Force passage with the party and its supplies intact. |
| Stakes | Defeat may permit capture, confiscation, or forced retreat within the declared intent. Victory does not automatically kill anyone. Establish any lethal stakes separately before resolution. |
| Participants | Venn Carrow, Edda Pike, Noll Sable |
| Starting opposition Disposition | Sum of the selected cards’ Grit: 9, 11, or 13 |
| Starting Woe | 6 |
| Opposition script | Noll Maneuver → Venn Attack → Edda Defend, repeated every exchange |
| Information purchases | Prohibited for Collectors |
| Paid script changes | Prohibited for Collectors |
| Selected Specials | Use whenever legally available, respecting costs, timing, limits, and fictional applicability |
| Other controls | No Superiority, reinforcements, or voluntary concessions in the calibration batch |

Grit contributes to shared Disposition; it is not personal health. None of these cards grants Boss Phases.

### Venn’s trait and Special

- Dead Aim: a free bonus die on his first Attack each scene. It counts toward the normal maximum of two bonus dice; it does not refresh each exchange.
- Volley: pay 2 Woe to make his permitted Attack an area Attack. Establish its area and everyone it can reach before resolving it. It does not automatically distinguish allies from enemies.
- When Volley applies ordinary Disposition loss, remove committed Power + 1 once, plus 1 for each additional opposing participant affected. It does not automatically inflict wounded or another status.

The carbine grants no additional damage bonus, ammunition subsystem, automatic ranged advantage, or weapon quality beyond what is explicitly implemented.

### Edda’s trait and Special

- Interpose: once per exchange, intercept an ally’s eligible imposed status before the original target applies Resistance or harm reduction. Edda receives the entire status and uses her own eligible reduction.
- Interpose does not redirect shared Disposition loss, accepted injury trades, running-dry statuses, concession costs, or other protected payments.
- It requires a plausible interception. Edda cannot protect an ally from an area effect that already affects both of them.
- Dirty Trick: after an eligible success, pay 1 Woe to Impede the opposing side’s next scripted action. This applies −3 to that action’s roll, does not stack with an existing pending Impede, and expires if the action makes no roll.

Her mail supplies Armour 1 against eligible physical statuses. Her shield grants no additional Armour, automatic recovery, or unlisted interception benefit. Armour and Shrugs do not stack against one status application.

## Review boundary

These proposals complete the previously missing card fields, but they do not approve the cards or certify the engine. In particular:

- Edda’s Armour 1 is a new baseline choice, held constant across her two ranks.
- Named weapons add no optional distinctive qualities unless separately approved.
- Area reach and Interpose eligibility still depend on established circumstances; equipment descriptions are not automatic permissions.
- Weaknesses provide fictional hooks, not free script changes or predetermined hunter knowledge.

This proposed calibration set is suitable for a separate review document. It should not overwrite the original-card reference or be presented as the equipment used in historical findings.
