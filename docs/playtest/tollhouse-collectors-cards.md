### Role trait — Dead Aim

Free bonus die on Venn's first Attack each scene. This is the Shooter role trait, separate from his selected Special.

### Special — Volley

Cost: 2 Woe.

Venn's Attack is an area Attack. Establish its area and everyone it can reach; it does not automatically distinguish allies from enemies.

Resolve committed Power + 1 loss against opposing shared Disposition once, plus 1 per additional opposing participant affected. An ordinary conflict Volley does not automatically inflict a status.

### Role trait — Interpose

Once per exchange, Edda may intercept an ally's imposed status before Resistance or harm reduction, applying her own normal reduction instead.

- Does not redirect ordinary shared Disposition loss.
- Requires circumstances that let her intercept the harm.
- Cannot protect an ally from an area effect that already affects both of them.
- Cannot redirect accepted injury trades, running-dry statuses, concession costs, or other protected payments.
- Outside conflicts, she may intercept fitting harm without a separate frequency limit.

### Special — Dirty Trick

Cost: 1 Woe.

Any successful action may also Impede the opposing side's next scripted action: −3 to that action's roll.

Pending Impedes do not stack. The Impede expires if that action makes no roll. It is not a tiered status and does not penalize Resistance.

### Opening assignment

Defend in volley 3.

## Shared human traits and handling

- Can be talked to: Persuade, Intimidate, bribes, Argument, and Negotiation can apply.
- Ordinary adversaries do not track Sōma, Mētis, Psychē, or Pneuma pools or use Effort.
- Woe pays for bonus dice and Specials. Bonus dice are capped at +2, including free dice such as Dead Aim.
- Armour and Shrugs reduce eligible imposed statuses, not ordinary shared Disposition loss.
- Human Morale applies outside conflicts as specified by the Kind package; it does not create individual Disposition thresholds.
- The three role traits do not consume their Minor-rank Special allowance. Each Collector has one selected Special: Volley or Dirty Trick.

These are the original Minor cards only. No rank promotions, later-exchange scripts, or new equipment have been added.
